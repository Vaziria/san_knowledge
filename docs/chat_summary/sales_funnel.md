# Sales Funnel

Summary of a chat session (2026-09-17) explaining sales funnelling for the Indonesian market. This is general marketing knowledge, not sourced research: all numbers are illustrative examples, not Indonesian benchmarks.

## Definition

A **sales funnel** (*corong penjualan*) is the path a person takes from first hearing about a business to buying. Fewer people remain at each stage, which gives the funnel shape. **Sales funnelling** means building that path on purpose, measuring where people drop out, and fixing the worst drop-off first.

## Stages

| Stage | Customer's question | Tactics | Metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| TOFU: awareness | "I have a problem" / "that looks interesting" | Short video, KOL content, SEO, memes | Reach, impressions, CPM, watch rate |
| MOFU: interest and consideration | "Is this right for me? Can I trust the seller?" | Demos, testimonials, comparisons, free trials, lead magnets | CTR, cost per lead, add-to-cart, WhatsApp chats started |
| BOFU: action | "Is the price right? Fast shipping? COD? Is it genuine?" | Limited-time promos, free shipping vouchers, bundles, guarantees, COD, fast admin replies | Conversion rate, CPA, AOV, cart abandonment |
| Retention and advocacy | "Should I buy again or recommend it?" | Follow-ups, reorder reminders, member vouchers, review requests, referrals | Repeat purchase rate, LTV, rating, referrals |

The classic model is AIDA (Awareness, Interest, Desire, Action). Retention is often left out, but repeat customers cost far less than new ones.

## Funnel math

Stage conversion rates multiply. Illustrative example with a Rp 3,000,000 budget:

| Stage | People | Rate to the next stage |
|---|---|---|
| Saw the ad | 100,000 | |
| Clicked | 1,500 | 1.5% CTR |
| Viewed the product page | 1,200 | 80% |
| Added to cart | 180 | 15% |
| Checked out | 60 | 33% |
| Paid or received (COD) | 48 | 80% |

- CPA = 3,000,000 ÷ 48 ≈ Rp 62,500 per order.
- Doubling the CTR roughly doubles orders on the same budget; raising checkout from 33% to 50% gives about 1.5× the orders.
- Scale only when LTV exceeds CPA plus product and operating costs.

## Funnel types in Indonesia

- **Marketplace** (Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok Shop): content → listing → reviews → voucher → checkout. The platform owns most of the funnel and the customer data.
- **Live commerce**: short video → live session with demo, Q&A and flash promo → checkout within minutes. The host is the salesperson.
- **Ads-to-WhatsApp**: ad → WhatsApp chat → admin handles objections → order (COD or transfer). Common for D2C brands and UMKM; admin reply speed and follow-up decide conversion.
- **Landing page / lead generation** (services, B2B, courses, property): long cycle, relies on lead nurturing.
- **Webinar / education**: free content → free webinar → offer at the end.

## Building a funnel

1. Define the target customer and where they spend time.
2. Pick the final action (purchase, chat, sign-up, booking).
3. Map the stages backwards from that action.
4. Make separate content for each stage.
5. Set up tracking: Meta/TikTok pixels, UTM links, marketplace analytics, chat and order counts.
6. Run, measure, fix the biggest leak, repeat.
7. Retarget people who engaged or added to cart but didn't buy.

## Diagnosing leaks

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to try |
|---|---|---|
| Many views, few clicks | Weak hook, wrong audience | New hooks in the first 3 seconds, change targeting |
| Many clicks, few add-to-carts | Page doesn't match the ad, price shock, weak photos | Match the ad promise, add social proof, show the price earlier |
| Many carts, few checkouts | Shipping cost, payment options, slow site | Free shipping vouchers, COD, fewer checkout steps |
| Many chats, few orders | Slow or poor admin replies, no follow-up | Reply scripts, reply time targets, follow up on quiet chats |
| Many orders, many COD returns | Impulse buyers, wrong expectations | Confirm orders before shipping, clearer product info |
| Sales but no repeat orders | Product experience, no retention plan | Post-purchase messages, reorder reminders, loyalty vouchers |

## Common mistakes

- Only bottom-of-funnel ads ("BUY NOW 50% OFF") to cold audiences: costs rise and the brand looks cheap.
- Only top-of-funnel content: views and followers with no path to buying.
- No tracking, so the broken stage can't be found.
- Judging every campaign on final sales only.
- Ignoring retention and paying again for customers already won.

## Funnel vs. flywheel

The funnel treats the customer as the end point; the flywheel (popularised by HubSpot) treats satisfied customers as the engine that brings in new ones through reviews and referrals. In Indonesia, where reviews, word of mouth and WhatsApp group sharing carry weight, use the funnel to optimise acquisition and the flywheel to turn buyers into promoters.
