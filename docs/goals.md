# My Goals.

## Main Goal: Upper Class Income

Reach **upper class** as BPS defines it, and get there by starting a business.

- BPS sorts households by **spending per person per month**, not income. Upper class starts at 17× the poverty line.
- The poverty line was about Rp 609k per person per month in March 2025 (BPS, from general knowledge, not a tracked source). That puts the upper-class line at **about Rp 10.4M per person per month**. Update it when BPS publishes a new poverty line.
- The middle class is shrinking: 57.33M people (2019) → 46.7M (2025) (BPS via news, see [jasa_tukang.md](market_research/jasa_tukang.md#market-size-and-demand)).

**Target: Rp 30M take-home income / month (~Rp 360M a year).** That is upper class for a household of 2 people.

| Household | Spending at the line / month | Take-home income needed / month (saving 25–30%) | Per year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 person | Rp 10.4M | Rp 14–15M | ~Rp 175M |
| 2 people | Rp 21M | Rp 28–30M | ~Rp 350M |
| 4 people | Rp 42M | Rp 55–60M | ~Rp 700M |

Tax note: an individual business owner pays 0.5% final tax on revenue (PP 55/2022), and the first Rp 500M of revenue a year is exempt. Employees need about 10–20% more before tax.

## Business Candidates vs the Goal

What each idea needs to reach **Rp 30M profit / month** (upper class for 2 people). The numbers are rough estimates built from the research, and none of the ideas has been validated yet.

| Candidate | Research | What Rp 30M profit takes | Capital | Ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Local perfume brand, Rp 150–400k | [produk_100rb_500rb.md](market_research/produk_100rb_500rb.md#candidate-products) | ~15–20% net margin after fees (15–30%) and ads → Rp 150–200M sales ≈ 750–1,000 orders/month at Rp 200k | Medium (maklon MOQ, BPOM, halal from 17 Oct 2026) | High: sells nationally online |
| Local running shoes, Rp 250–500k | same | Similar sales, higher stock and size-run risk | Medium–high | High |
| Household goods / tukang tools | [produk_100rb_500rb.md](market_research/produk_100rb_500rb.md#farm-tools-tukang-tools-and-household-goods) | Thinner margins against Chinese value brands and retail chains → more volume | Low–medium | Medium |
| Jasa tukang, Blitar managed service | [jasa_tukang_blitar.md](market_research/jasa_tukang_blitar.md#assessment) | Small jobs alone are too small. Renovation projects (~Rp 30–50M each at Rp 0.9–1.5M/m²) at a 10–15% fee → 5–8 projects/month | Low | Low–medium in Blitar; grows with an East Java cluster |
| Maintenance for property owners in a bigger city | [jasa_tukang.md](market_research/jasa_tukang.md#product-options) | Recurring B2B contracts plus a vetted tukang bench | Low | Medium |

**Reading:**

- Online products have the **highest ceiling** because they are not limited to one city, but they need capital and have tight margins.
- Jasa tukang needs **little capital**, but Blitar alone caps income. It reaches the goal only through renovation projects or expanding to more cities.
- A possible path: run the low-capital tukang pilot for cash flow and learning (steps 1–2), while validating perfume or shoes as the scalable business for step 3.
