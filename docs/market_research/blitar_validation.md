# Blitar Validation Kit

Plan and scripts for cheaply testing the ideas in [the Blitar research](jasa_tukang_blitar.md) before building anything. Three tests, each doable over WhatsApp or in person in about two weeks. Scripts are in Indonesian; adjust to Javanese (*boso*) politeness where it fits.

## Hypotheses

| # | Hypothesis | Test | Passes if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Local providers can be recruited at prices that leave a margin | Provider price survey | At least 5 of 10 providers share prices, and at least 3 would accept regular jobs with a 15–20% cut |
| H2 | Migrant-worker (PMI) families renovate from abroad and fear being cheated | PMI family interviews | At least 4 of 8 families renovated in the last 3 years or plan to in the next year, and at least 3 had a problem (overpaying, delays, disappearing tukang) |
| H3 | Local businesses would pay a monthly maintenance fee | Business owner interviews | At least 3 of 10 say they would try a paid plan at Rp 150–300k a month, or already pay someone regularly |

Record honest answers, including "no". A failed hypothesis is a useful result.

## Test 1: Provider Price Survey

**Who:** 10 providers: 5 AC service shops (e.g. from the [AC shop list](../external_sources/web/tokoterdekat.dapodik.co.id/service-ac-blitar.md)), 3 independent tukang (Facebook groups, tukangblitar.com, referrals from a *toko bangunan*), 2 plumbers or electricians.

**How:** introduce yourself honestly as someone planning a local home-service business. Providers may prefer to give prices for a real job, so if you have one at home, ask for a quote for it.

**WhatsApp script (opening):**

> Assalamualaikum / Selamat pagi, Pak/Mas. Saya [nama] dari Blitar. Saya sedang merencanakan usaha jasa perbaikan rumah di Blitar dan ingin bekerja sama dengan tukang/teknisi lokal. Boleh saya tanya sedikit soal tarif dan cara kerja Bapak? Kira-kira 5 menit saja.

**Questions:**

1. Layanan apa saja yang biasa Bapak kerjakan?
2. Berapa tarif untuk: cuci AC 1/2–1 PK, isi freon, pasang AC baru, perbaikan pipa bocor, tambah stop kontak? (Sesuaikan dengan keahliannya.)
3. Kalau harian, berapa per hari untuk tukang dan kenek? Kalau borongan, biasanya dihitung bagaimana?
4. Ada biaya cek/survei? Ada garansi? Berapa lama?
5. Biasanya dapat pelanggan dari mana? (Teman, WA, Facebook, toko bangunan, aplikasi?)
6. Dalam seminggu, kira-kira berapa pekerjaan? Masih ada waktu kosong?
7. Kalau ada yang mengirim pekerjaan rutin dengan harga tetap, apakah Bapak tertarik? Potongan berapa persen yang masih wajar menurut Bapak?

**Record:**

| Provider | Type | Area | Cuci AC | Freon | Harian tukang / kenek | Survey fee | Warranty | Lead source | Jobs/week | Open to partnering? | Acceptable cut |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | | | | |

## Test 2: PMI Family Interviews

**Who:** 8 families with a member working abroad (Hong Kong, Taiwan, Malaysia, Korea). Find them through village offices (*kantor desa*), PMI communities, recruitment agency (P3MI) contacts, or personal networks. Start in the regency districts with the most migrant workers: Ponggok, Kademangan, Gandusari, and Wonodadi (Desa Kolomayan). Where possible, also speak to the worker abroad over WhatsApp call.

**Opening:**

> Selamat siang, Bu/Pak. Saya [nama], sedang meneliti kebutuhan renovasi rumah untuk keluarga yang anggotanya bekerja di luar negeri. Tidak ada yang saya jual. Boleh saya bertanya sekitar 15 menit tentang pengalaman Ibu/Bapak?

**Questions** (ask about real past events, not hypotheticals, until the last two):

1. Siapa yang bekerja di luar negeri, di negara mana, sudah berapa lama?
2. Dalam 3 tahun terakhir, apakah rumah dibangun atau direnovasi? Pekerjaan apa saja? Kira-kira habis berapa?
3. Siapa yang mengatur pekerjaannya di sini? Bagaimana mencari tukangnya?
4. Bagaimana cara pembayarannya: uang muka berapa persen, dibayar bertahap atau borongan?
5. Bagaimana yang di luar negeri memantau pekerjaannya? (Foto, video call, kabar dari keluarga?)
6. Ada masalah? Misalnya biaya membengkak, pekerjaan molor, hasil tidak sesuai, tukang menghilang. Ceritakan.
7. Apakah ada rencana renovasi lagi dalam setahun ke depan? Pekerjaan apa?
8. Kalau ada layanan yang mengatur tukang, mengirim foto/video progres tiap minggu, dan pembayaran bertahap hanya setelah tiap tahap selesai, apakah menarik? Kenapa?
9. Biaya jasa berapa persen dari total yang masih wajar menurut Ibu/Bapak?

**Record:** country, relationship, renovation in last 3 years (yes/no, type, spend), who managed it, payment method, how it was monitored, problems (quote them), next renovation plan, reaction to the offer, acceptable fee.

## Test 3: Business Owner Interviews

**Who:** 10 owners: 3 shops or minimarkets, 2 clinics or pharmacies, 2 homestays or guesthouses (near Makam Bung Karno), 2 egg farms (Kabupaten), 1 kos owner.

**Opening:**

> Selamat siang, Pak/Bu. Saya [nama], sedang meneliti kebutuhan perawatan bangunan untuk usaha di Blitar. Boleh saya tanya sebentar, sekitar 10 menit?

**Questions:**

1. Dalam 6 bulan terakhir, perbaikan apa saja yang dibutuhkan? (AC, listrik, pipa, atap bocor, cat, pompa air, instalasi kandang?)
2. Siapa yang mengerjakan? Bagaimana menghubunginya? Berapa lama sampai datang?
3. Kira-kira berapa total biaya perbaikan dan perawatan per bulan atau per tahun?
4. Pernah ada masalah? Tukang tidak datang, hasil jelek, harga tidak jelas?
5. Apakah sekarang sudah membayar seseorang secara rutin untuk perawatan (misalnya servis AC berkala)?
6. Kalau ada paket bulanan Rp 150–300 ribu yang mencakup cek rutin (AC, listrik, air) dan panggilan darurat dengan harga tetap, apakah mau mencoba? Apa yang harus termasuk supaya layak?

**Record:** business type, size, repairs in the last 6 months, current provider, yearly spend, problems, already paying regularly (yes/no, how much), reaction to the plan, must-have items.

## Decision

After the three tests, update [the Blitar research](jasa_tukang_blitar.md) with the results and choose:

| Result | Next step |
|---|---|
| H1 and H3 pass | Start a WhatsApp-first maintenance service for businesses with 3–5 partner providers |
| H1 and H2 pass | Pilot remote renovation management for PMI families with 1–2 projects |
| Only H1 passes | Consider a fixed-price consumer menu, or test another city (Kediri, Malang) |
| H1 fails | Rethink: providers won't partner at workable prices |
