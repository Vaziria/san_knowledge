# Dropship on Shopee

Research done 2026-09-17 on whether a beginner with no warehouse can sell on Shopee by dropshipping, what Shopee's store-performance rules mean for a dropshipper, and where to find suppliers. Shopee's own Seller Centre pages ([7070](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7070), [6868](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/6868), [339](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/339)) need JavaScript and could not be read, so **the penalty rules below come from seller-tool and consultant blogs that cite Shopee (secondary)**. Claims seen only in search results are marked **(snippet)**.

## Summary

- **Dropship is allowed on Shopee.** Shopee's own blog explains it, and checkout has a dropshipper option: the label and order details show the dropshipper as sender, without the Shopee logo ([Shopee Inspirasi](../external_sources/web/shopee.co.id/inspirasi-shopee-cara-menjadi-dropship-di-shopee.md)).
- **The risk is your store's health, not the rules.** Shopee scores the store weekly. A late-shipping rate of 10% or more, or 10% or more unfinished orders (cancelled or returned because of the seller), each cost a penalty point. At 3 points the store loses promotions and drops in search ([Ordal](../external_sources/web/ordal.co.id/poin-penalti-shopee.md), [BigSeller](../external_sources/web/bigseller.com/blog-articledetails-4163-sistem-poin-penalti-shopee.md)). A slow or out-of-stock supplier puts those points on **your** store.
- **Moving buyers to WhatsApp is penalised.** Inviting a transaction outside Shopee, or putting personal contacts, links or social media in the store, costs 2 points. This limits the "follow up on WhatsApp" advice in [Sales Funnel](../chat_summary/sales_funnel.md) for Shopee buyers.
- **Fake orders are heavily penalised** (3–9 points), so a new store can't "seed" reviews with fake buyers.
- **You pay the supplier first.** Dropship still needs some working capital; one seller-tool blog suggests about Rp 500k to start ([Jubelio](../external_sources/web/jubelio.com/cara-dropship-di-shopee.md)).
- **Suppliers exist for household goods and tools**, and there are virtual warehouses in Surabaya that repack supplier parcels under your label, but none of their prices or terms were checked.
- **National dropship platforms that fit a Shopee store: Crewdible (free, COD) and Jakmall (Rp 350k to join).** DropshipAja has its own perfume brand. Several platforms that online lists still recommend are gone: Dusdusan's domain now hosts a game server, Selleri's domain is dead, and Raena's website shows a gambling page.

## How It Works

Two setups (Jubelio, Shopee Inspirasi):

1. **Your own store, you forward orders.** List the supplier's products, and when a buyer orders, pass the order to the supplier. The supplier might be a Shopee store, a wholesaler or a reseller platform. You can ask the supplier to put your store name on the shipping label.
2. **Shopee to Shopee.** You buy from the supplier's Shopee store, tick the dropshipper option at checkout and enter your buyer's address. On the website the option is under the shipping address; in the app it is at the bottom. The supplier sees the order marked "Dropshipper" in Seller Centre.

In both setups you pay the supplier before Shopee releases the buyer's payment to you.

## Shopee Penalty Points

Source: [Ordal](../external_sources/web/ordal.co.id/poin-penalti-shopee.md) (a marketplace consultant's blog), consistent with [BigSeller](../external_sources/web/bigseller.com/blog-articledetails-4163-sistem-poin-penalti-shopee.md). Store health is evaluated weekly and points update every Monday. They show in Seller Centre under Kesehatan Toko → Penalti Saya.

### Rules that matter for a dropshipper

| Breach | Points |
|---|---|
| Unfinished orders ≥10% (cancelled/returned within 7 days due to seller) | 1 |
| Unfinished orders ≥10% and ≥30 orders | 2 |
| Unfinished orders ≥60% (<30 orders / ≥30 orders) | 3 / 4 |
| Late shipping ≥10% (shipped after the deadline on the product page) | 1 |
| Late shipping ≥10% and ≥50 late orders | 2 |
| Asking the buyer to cancel via chat | 2 |
| Inviting a transaction outside Shopee | 2, or account restriction |
| Personal contact, links or social media in the store | 2 |
| Empty or wrong parcel (1st / 2nd / 3rd+) | 6 / 9 / restriction |
| Fake orders or buyers | 3–9 plus extra sanctions |
| Imitation, inaccurate or brand-distribution violations | 1–2, product removed |
| Pre-order products >20% (stores with ≥300 products) | display limit |

### Penalty tiers

| Points | Sanctions |
|---|---|
| <3 | None |
| 3–11 (tiers I–III) | No Shopee promotions; products ranked lower on home, category and search |
| 12–14 (tier IV) | As above, plus no uploading or editing products |
| ≥15 (tier V) | As above, plus account restriction |

BigSeller adds that each 3 points of product violations limits the store to 100 displayed products for 28 days, and pre-order violations limit it to 500 products for 7 days.

### What this means for a dropshipper (inference)

- **One bad supplier week can cost a point.** For a new store with few orders, 1 late order in 10 already reaches the 10% line. Three such weeks put the store into tier I, out of promotions and lower in search, exactly when it needs visibility.
- **Wrong items shipped by the supplier count as your "wrong parcel".** A supplier that sends the wrong variant is a 6-point risk.
- **Set the processing time you can really keep**, based on the supplier's real speed, not the fastest option.
- **Out-of-stock means a cancellation**, which counts toward unfinished orders. Check supplier stock daily.

### Crewdible in detail

Crewdible is a **fulfillment (online warehouse) company**, not a shop. It was founded in 2017 in Tangerang by Dhana Galindra and Sony Gultom and has raised about USD 6.3M, including from Bukalapak and Global Founders Capital (Tracxn/Crunchbase, snippet; USD 1.5M pre-Series A in Oct 2019 per [e27](https://e27.co/crewdible-gets-us1-5m-pre-series-a-funding-round-to-help-small-businesses-find-warehouses-20191029/)). It started by turning idle space (homes, offices, small warehouses) into micro fulfillment centres, and now claims **170+ warehouses** across Indonesia ([Instant Fulfillment](../external_sources/web/crewdible.com/instant-fulfillment.md), [About](../external_sources/web/crewdible.com/tentang.md)).

It runs two sides:

1. **Dropshipper Network** ([supplier side](../external_sources/web/crewdible.com/dropshipper-network.md), [dropshipper side](../external_sources/web/crewdible.com/dropshipper.md)). Brands and suppliers send stock to Crewdible's warehouses and list it; dropshippers pick products and sell them. The warehouse packs and ships. Because stock is already in a warehouse with stock sync, the late-shipping and out-of-stock risks are lower than with a supplier who packs by hand. Crewdible says 75% of orders come from outside Jakarta.
2. **Instant Fulfillment** for sellers with their own stock. The seller connects their marketplace stores, sends stock in, and Crewdible picks, packs (bubble wrap or poly mailer included) and ships.

| Package | Fee | Fits | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lite** | **5% of GMV**, minimum Rp 2,000 per order, no maximum | 50–300 SKU, 1–500 orders/month, 1–5 CBM | Shared CS, warranty 50% of selling price, 100% QC counting, free stock count once a year |
| **Plus** | **5.5% of GMV**, minimum Rp 3,000, no maximum | >300 SKU, >500 orders/month, >5 CBM | Dedicated CS, warranty 75%, barcode, CCTV, insurance included |

Also: cold storage, returns handling, order management across marketplaces, P&L and stock reports.

**What this means for us (inference):**

- **Stage 1, dropship:** use the Dropshipper Network to test household goods at no cost.
- **Stage 3, own brand (e.g. perfume from maklon):** Instant Fulfillment answers "I have no warehouse". On a Rp 200k perfume the fee is about **Rp 10k per order** (5%), on top of Shopee's 10–25%. At the goal volume (750–1,000 orders/month, [My Goals](../goals.md)) the store would be on Plus (5.5%, about Rp 11k per order), roughly Rp 8–11M a month. Compare with packing at home.
- Not checked: storage fees beyond the GMV fee and user reviews (none found in search).

### Crewdible Dropshipper Network: the terms

Source: the dropshipper [terms and conditions](../external_sources/web/dms.crewdible.com/assets-pdf-toc.md) (PDF, undated), plus [DailySocial](../external_sources/web/cms.dailysocial.id/post-crewdible-dropship.md). The operator is **PT Duta Daya Digital**, through dms.crewdible.com and an Android app.

**Joining**

- Free (Freemium) or paid (Premium) membership. The Premium price isn't in the terms, and fees paid are non-refundable.
- Lifetime account with **no minimum transactions** (unlike Jakmall's 1 order a month).
- Sign-up needs email, name, WhatsApp number (OTP is sent by WhatsApp) and password. The terms also require full name, address, date of birth, **KTP, NPWP and bank account**.

**How an order flows**

1. Connect your marketplace store (Shopee, Lazada, Tokopedia named) and **clone** Crewdible products into it. Orders from the store are handled automatically.
2. Orders from buyers outside a marketplace (e.g. WhatsApp) are entered manually by the dropshipper and are **fully the dropshipper's responsibility**.
3. **Crewdible only ships once it has received the dropshipper price from you** (C-2.7, C-2.12). Even for marketplace orders you pay first, while Shopee pays you only after delivery. You need a working balance.

**Price and margin**

- Each product has a **"harga dropshipper"** (your cost) and a **"harga ke konsumen"** (consumer price). Your commission is the difference.
- The consumer price in the catalogue "is the price that must be shown to buyers" (C-2.2). The supplier-side page says dropshippers sell at "the same or higher" consumer price. So **you can't undercut**, and on Shopee your margin is the listed gap minus Shopee's fees, unless you price higher.
- Prices and commissions can change at any time. Search snippets mention discounts or commission "up to 50%" (unverified).

**Shipping**

- A third-party courier ships; shipping cost follows Crewdible/courier policy.
- **No guarantee of on-time shipping** (C-4.3). Shopee late-shipping penalties still fall on your store.

**Returns**

- Within **2 days of receipt**, unused, tags intact, original packaging, **with an unboxing video**. Request via Crewdible CS on WhatsApp first.
- Not returnable: jewellery, underwear, swimwear, socks, cosmetics (unless expired), and items that match the listed size.
- Replacement if in stock; otherwise credit to your account, refund 2 working days after the item reaches the warehouse. Returns usually arrive within 5 working days.
- Return shipping and bank transfer costs caused by the dropshipper's negligence are yours.

**Liability**

- Crewdible disclaims product quality, descriptions, shipping delays and cancellations, and disputes with buyers (B-2, B-18). In practice the dropshipper carries the risk towards the buyer.

**What this means for a Shopee dropshipper (inference)**

- **Cash flow:** keep a deposit big enough for several days of orders, since you pay Crewdible before Shopee pays you. COD orders make the gap longer, and a refused COD parcel is a return you may pay for.
- **Return mismatch:** Crewdible accepts returns within 2 days with an unboxing video. A Shopee buyer who complains later, or has no video, may get a refund from Shopee that you can't pass back to Crewdible. Price that risk in, or avoid fragile and size-sensitive products.
- **Margin check before listing:** for each product, consumer price − dropshipper price − Shopee fee (about 10–25%) must stay positive. If the catalogue gap is thin, it won't survive Shopee's fees.
- **Where stock ships from matters for speed:** products ship from the supplier's Crewdible warehouse. Mekari Jurnal lists Crewdible warehouses in Surabaya (Margomulyo) and **Malang (Sawojajar)**, about 80 km from Blitar ([Jurnal](../external_sources/web/jurnal.id/id-blog-crewdible-gudang-online-untuk-dropship.md), blog, fees there look like an older pricing scheme). Prefer products stored in East Java.
- **Still to check in the app:** real margins in the household category, the Premium price and what it adds, top-up and withdrawal rules, and whether Shopee store connection works in 2026.

### Which platform for which goal (inference)

- **Household goods on your own Shopee store:** Crewdible first (free, COD, stock sync), Jakmall second (paid, no COD per its help page).
- **Learning the perfume category:** DropshipAja's Summerscent, or Raena if it still operates. Also see how a local perfume brand prices for resellers.
- **Selling to friends, family and WhatsApp groups without a store:** Evermos.
- Before paying any registration fee, test-order 2–3 products to your own address, and check the site still works: a closed platform can leave your listings pointing at stock that no longer exists.

## Fit With Our Candidate Products

Inference, building on [Produk Rp 100–500 Ribu](produk_100rb_500rb.md) and [My Goals](../goals.md):

- **Household goods:** best fit. Surabaya has several suppliers, it's close to Blitar, and buyers search on Shopee (Shopee has a 69% homecare share in FMCG e-commerce, [Katadata](../external_sources/web/katadata.co.id/digital-e-commerce-6a882591bc6de-belanja-kebutuhan-harian-makin-pindah-676c12.md), via news).
- **Tukang tools:** workable through distributors like Sakha, but imitation and brand-distribution rules are a penalty risk with branded tools.
- **Running shoes:** weak fit. Size stock changes fast, so cancellations and wrong-size parcels are likely.
- **Own perfume brand:** not dropship. It needs maklon and own stock. Dropshipping existing local perfume brands could still be used to learn the category.

## Suggested Approach

1. Pick 1–2 Surabaya-area suppliers for household goods and place test orders to your own address: check speed, packing and accuracy.
2. List 10–20 products, and set the processing time to the supplier's real speed plus a buffer.
3. Check supplier stock daily and track late, cancelled and wrong orders weekly against the 10% lines.
4. If a supplier's parcels carry their branding or you use several suppliers, compare the cost of a Surabaya virtual warehouse repack against your margin.
5. Once a product sells steadily, move it to small stock at home.
