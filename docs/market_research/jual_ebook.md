# Selling Ebooks in Indonesia

Research done 2026-09-17 on whether selling an ebook is a workable digital product in Indonesia: who reads ebooks, where to sell them, what the platforms charge, what goes wrong (piracy, marketplace rules), and tax. Several pages could not be read: Shopee's Seller Centre (needs JavaScript), Lynk.id's pricing page (403) and Mayar's fee table (rendered by JavaScript). **Fees for those three come from secondary blogs or search snippets and are marked as such.** Claims seen only in search results are marked **(snippet)**.

## Summary

- **Ebooks are a second format, not the main one.** In a 2025 GoodStats online survey, 85.2% read print most often, 43.1% read ebooks, and 81.5% want to keep reading print ([GoodStats](../external_sources/web/goodstats.id/article-survei-goodstats-masih-gemarkah-publik-indonesia-baca-buku-di-547379.md)). The sample skews young (40.2% aged 18–25).
- **Self-development and non-fiction are what people want to read,** ahead of education and fiction. That fits a "how-to" ebook better than a novel.
- **Discovery is social.** 62.5% find new books through social media, and reviews/recommendations are the top purchase factor (39%). 77.9% say online reviews drive their decision.
- **Sell through a creator checkout page, not a marketplace.** Lynk.id and Mayar take QRIS, e-wallets and bank transfer and deliver the file automatically. Shopee allows ebooks only in "Tiket, Voucher & Layanan", without COD, with manual delivery through chat and a possible "official seller" check (secondary).
- **Piracy is the main risk.** A Kompas investigation (May 2026) found the bestseller *Filosofi Teras* sold as a PDF for Rp 3,000 via QRIS and Google Drive ([IKAPI, reprinting Kompas](../external_sources/web/ikapi.org/2026-05-30-pembajak-buku-bunuh-intelektualisme-indonesia.md)). Copyright cases only proceed if the rights holder files a complaint.
- **Google Play Books pays less in Indonesia.** The 70% revenue share does **not** apply to Indonesian buyers; Indonesia is on the standard share, which defaults to 52% ([Google](../external_sources/web/support.google.com/books-partner-answer-9331459-hl-id.md)). Some Indonesian blogs say otherwise.
- **Tax is light for a small seller.** An individual's first Rp 500 million a year of turnover is not taxed; above that, final PPh is 0.5% of turnover, and since PP 20/2026 that rate has no time limit for individuals ([DJP](../external_sources/web/pajak.go.id/en-node-119950.md)).

## Who Reads Ebooks

Source: [GoodStats survey "Preferensi Membaca Buku di Era Digital 2025"](../external_sources/web/goodstats.id/article-survei-goodstats-masih-gemarkah-publik-indonesia-baca-buku-di-547379.md), online, 1,000 respondents, 20 Jan–10 Feb 2025, 64% from Java.

| Finding | Figure |
|---|---|
| Read books every day / week / month | 20.7% / 22.3% / 24.6% |
| Read print most often | 85.2% |
| Read ebooks | 43.1% |
| Read audiobooks | 7.1% |
| Want to keep reading print | 81.5% |
| Find new books through social media | 62.5% |
| Main purchase factor: recommendations and reviews | 39% |
| Want engaging social media content as publisher marketing | 33.3% |

Favourite genres, in order: self-development, non-fiction, education, fiction. (A search snippet puts self-development at 65%.)

Caveats: an online survey of mostly young respondents will overstate reading and ebook use compared with the whole population. The survey doesn't say how many people **pay** for ebooks.

## What Sells (secondary)

No sales data was found for ebooks. Creator-platform blogs, which have an interest in the answer, list these as popular digital products:

- Practical ebooks: recipes, parenting, motivation, study material ([Linkreator](../external_sources/web/blog.linkreator.id/produk-digital-yang-bisa-dijual-di-shopee.md))
- Printable worksheets for preschool/early primary, Rp 20k–100k per set **(snippet)**
- Budget/financial planners in PDF or Excel, Rp 30k–150k **(snippet)**
- Canva, PowerPoint and CV templates

Lynk.id storefronts show digital products priced from about Rp 3k to Rp 749k **(snippet)**. The Linkreator article uses Rp 75,000 as an example ebook price.

Inference: printable worksheets and planners are ebook-adjacent and harder to pirate usefully than a plain-text PDF, because buyers pay for design and ready-to-print layout.

### Example margin (inference)

A Rp 49,000 ebook on Lynk.id's free plan keeps about Rp 46,550 after the 5% fee. Withdrawing weekly costs Rp 5,000 each time, so at 10 sales a week the take-home is about Rp 460,500 (~94%). The real cost is getting buyers: ads or content time, which this research did not price.

## Piracy

Source: [IKAPI reprint of the Kompas investigation, 18 May 2026](../external_sources/web/ikapi.org/2026-05-30-pembajak-buku-bunuh-intelektualisme-indonesia.md).

- Pirated books are sold openly in Blok M, Senen and Kwitang (Jakarta) and online. Print piracy runs through agents, printers in Kalibaru (Senen) and resellers.
- **Ebooks:** an Instagram account sold *Filosofi Teras* as a PDF for Rp 3,000, paid by QRIS and delivered as a Google Drive folder. Elsewhere the PDF sells for Rp 3,000–10,000. The file was made from phone scans (CamScanner).
- Telegram channels share free PDFs of Indonesian books **(snippet)**.
- Law No. 28/2014 on Copyright: up to 10 years' prison and/or a Rp 4 billion fine (Kompas; a snippet says Rp 5 billion). It is a complaint-based offence (delik aduan), so the rights holder must report. IKAPI's chair says many publishers can't afford to; the author Maman Suherman gave up because new pirate accounts keep appearing.

What this means for a seller (inference):
- Expect any successful PDF to leak. Price and position it so buyers pay for things a leak doesn't carry: updates, a community/WhatsApp group, templates, a consult or a bundle.
- Put the buyer's name/email on each copy (most checkout tools can't do this; check).
- Cheap "big bundle of 1,000 ebooks" offers compete directly on price; don't try to win there.

## Tax

Sources: [DJP, seller e-commerce tax](../external_sources/web/pajak.go.id/index-php-en-node-116878.md), [DJP, PP 20/2026](../external_sources/web/pajak.go.id/en-node-119950.md). These are articles by DJP staff, stated as personal opinion.

- An individual with turnover up to Rp 4.8 billion a year can use **final PPh 0.5% of turnover**.
- **The first Rp 500 million of turnover a year is not taxed** (individuals only). At Rp 600 million, tax is 0.5% × Rp 100 million = Rp 500,000.
- **PP 20/2026** (in force 22 April 2026) removes the time limit for individuals and one-person companies (perseroan perorangan). Newly registered CV, firma and PT can no longer use it. Turnover of husband, wife and their one-person companies is combined.
- Even below Rp 500 million, the seller must have an NPWP and file the annual SPT.
- VAT on ebooks: a snippet says educational books, print or electronic, can be VAT-free while novels, comics and entertainment books are not. **Not checked** against the regulation; it matters only for a PKP (VAT-registered) seller.

## Fit With Our Projects (inference)

- **Blitar builder services ([jasa_tukang_blitar.md](jasa_tukang_blitar.md)):** a cheap or free guide ("renovation checklist and cost estimates for Blitar") works better as a lead magnet than as a product. It builds trust with the 40+ homeowner audience and collects WhatsApp contacts.
- **Rp 100–500k products ([produk_100rb_500rb.md](produk_100rb_500rb.md)):** an ebook doesn't need stock, COD or shipping, which removes the cash, returns and penalty risks in [Dropship on Shopee](dropship_shopee.md). But the typical price is far below that band, so revenue depends on volume or bundles.
- **Audience ([online_audience.md](online_audience.md)):** TikTok reach and e-wallet/QRIS use support selling from content to a checkout link. COD preference (68.2% of online shoppers) and scam worries work against paying up front for a file, so testimonials, a free sample chapter and a known face matter.

## Suggested Approach

1. Pick a practical niche where we have real expertise (renovation, selling on Shopee/TikTok, household budgeting), not general motivation.
2. Validate before writing: post short TikTok/Instagram content on the topic and offer a free checklist via Lynk.id to count sign-ups.
3. Launch on Lynk.id's free plan at around Rp 49–99k, with a sample chapter and a bonus (template or WhatsApp group) that a pirated PDF won't include.
4. Add Google Play Books later only for reach, accepting the lower share.
5. Keep turnover records from day one and register an NPWP; tax stays zero until Rp 500 million a year.
