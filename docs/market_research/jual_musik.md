# Selling Music in Indonesia

Research done 2026-09-17 on whether selling music can be a business in Indonesia. It looks at four ways to earn: streaming through a distributor, stock/production music libraries, background-music licences for businesses, and made-to-order jingles. It also covers what the world market for instrumental music is worth, royalty rules (LMKN) and tax. Several pages could not be read: Pond5's payout page and Envato's earnings help page (both 403), and the paid IFPI and Research and Markets reports. **Figures for those come from search snippets and are marked (snippet).** Currency conversions use about Rp 16,000 per USD and are estimates.

## Summary

- **No one publishes an "instrumental music" revenue figure.** The world recorded music market was **USD 31.7 billion in 2025** ([IFPI](../external_sources/web/ifpi.org/global-music-report-2026-global-recorded-music-revenues-grow-6-4-as-re-92d618.md)). The instrumental-heavy parts can only be estimated: royalty-free/stock music at about **USD 1.5 billion** (a paid report's teaser), and functional music (sleep, focus, lo-fi) at about **USD 630 million a year for recording rights holders** (Endel's 2022 estimate).
- **Indonesia streams a lot but pays little per stream.** Indonesians streamed 32 billion times in 2025, but only about 18% of streaming users pay ([detik, citing Luminate](../external_sources/web/detik.com/pop-music-d-8375223-fans-indonesia-putar-musik-32-miliar-kali-sepanjang-2025.md)). Spotify pays about USD 0.003 per stream on average, and less for Indonesian listeners **(snippet)**.
- **Streaming alone is a weak route to the Rp 30M/month goal.** At Indonesian rates that takes roughly 1–2.5 million streams a month (inference, see [Streaming](#streaming)). A track earns nothing below 1,000 streams a year, and Spotify pays noise recordings less ([Spotify](../external_sources/web/artists.spotify.com/blog-modernizing-our-royalty-system.md)).
- **The Indonesian opening is background music for businesses.** Since the Mie Gacoan case (2025), cafés know they must pay LMKN about **Rp 120,000 per seat per year** even when they play Spotify ([ANTARA](../external_sources/web/antaranews.com/berita-5024069-rincian-biaya-royalti-musik-untuk-kafe-restoran-serta-c-4d324f.md)). Some stopped playing music. DJKI itself lists royalty-free music and **direct permission from independent musicians** as legal alternatives ([DJKI](../external_sources/web/dgip.go.id/artikel-detail-artikel-berita-mempelajari-kewajiban-bayar-royalti-untu-8a81cd.md)).
- **But "instrumental" does not mean "royalty-free".** LMKN says instrumental music and even professionally recorded nature sounds played in a business still owe royalties ([Tempo](../external_sources/web/tempo.co/hukum-polemik-royalti-lagu-para-musikus-bebaskan-karya-mereka-diputar-46cefd.md)). A seller must own 100% of the rights and give each business a written licence.
- **YouTube works better as a shop window than an income source.** Music ads pay little (about Rp 3,200 per 1,000 Indonesian views, snippet). YouTube won't pay channels of templated or AI-made loops ([policy](../external_sources/web/support.google.com/youtube-answer-1311392-hl-en.md)). New channels need twice the watch hours from 1 Feb 2027 ([YouTube](../external_sources/web/blog.youtube/news-and-events-youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn.md)).
- **Jingles for small businesses are cheap and crowded.** Sribu and Fastwork list jingles from about Rp 100,000 ([Sribu](../external_sources/web/sribu.com/id-music-audio-jingle-production.md)). That works for cash flow, not scale.

## World Market for Instrumental Music

No public source measures instrumental music as a category. IFPI, Luminate and Spotify report by format, region or genre, not by "has vocals or not". The table puts together the parts where instrumental music is most of what is sold.

| Segment | Size | Source and reliability |
|---|---|---|
| All recorded music, world, 2025 | USD 31.7B (+6.4%); streaming 69.6%, paid subscriptions 52.4%, performance rights USD 2.9B | [IFPI Global Music Report 2026](../external_sources/web/ifpi.org/global-music-report-2026-global-recorded-music-revenues-grow-6-4-as-re-92d618.md), industry data (reliable) |
| Royalty-free music, 2025 → 2030 | USD 1.52B → 2.03B (5.9% CAGR) | [Research and Markets teaser](../external_sources/web/globenewswire.com/news-release-2025-03-14-3042948-28124-en-opportunities-in-the-royalty-12afb8.md), paid report, method unknown |
| Stock music, 2030 | USD 2.82B (4.5% CAGR) | OpenPR press release **(snippet)**; a different definition from the row above |
| Functional music on streaming (sleep, focus, rain, ambient, lo-fi) | ~120 billion streams a year ≈ USD 630M+ a year to recording rights holders (2022) | Endel's estimate, reported by [Billboard](../external_sources/web/billboard.com/pro-ai-spotify-streaming-background-functional-music.md). Endel sells functional music, so the estimate isn't neutral. Spotify says functional music is "a very small amount" of its streams ([Spotify](../external_sources/web/support.spotify.com/us-artists-article-functional-music.md)) |
| Epidemic Sound (largest royalty-free library) | SEK 1,828M revenue in 2025 (≈ USD 170–190M), +3% in constant currency; +14% in Q2 2026 | [Epidemic Group annual report release](../external_sources/web/news.cision.com/epidemic-sound-r-epidemic-group-publishes-2025-annual-and-sustainabili-6fd04d.md) (company figures) |

What this says (inference):
- Instrumental music for **use in content and businesses** (royalty-free, stock, production music) is a market of about USD 1.5–3B worldwide. That is roughly 5–10% of recorded music revenue, and it grows at about 5–6% a year.
- Epidemic Sound alone is about a tenth of the royalty-free estimate, and it pays artists through a **50/50 revenue share plus upfront payments**. Envato, Pond5, Artlist and Musicbed are the other main buyers of tracks.
- Growth is under pressure from **AI**. Epidemic is turning itself into an "AI-first" platform, and Billboard describes AI companies making functional music at scale. Generic background tracks will get cheaper. Music made to a customer's order and local styles hold value better.

## Ways to Earn

### Streaming

Upload through a distributor to Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, TikTok and others.

| Distributor | Price | Artist share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Netrilis** (Yogyakarta, since 2015) | Rp 99,900 per song (Basic) or Rp 114,900 (all platforms), no yearly fee | 75% | Includes YouTube Content ID claims; you keep 100% of the copyright ([Netrilis](../external_sources/web/netrilis.com/p-harga.md)) |
| Musicblast.id, DistroKid, TuneCore, CD Baby | — | — | Not checked in this research |

Spotify rules that matter for instrumental music ([Spotify, 2023 announcement](../external_sources/web/artists.spotify.com/blog-modernizing-our-royalty-system.md)):
- A track needs at least **1,000 streams in 12 months** to earn anything. Tens of millions of tracks below that averaged USD 0.03 a month.
- Noise recordings (white noise, nature sounds, ASMR) must be at least **2 minutes long** to earn, and are paid "a fraction" of a music stream.
- Spotify openly says background and production music "tend to command lower royalty rates" ([Spotify](../external_sources/web/support.spotify.com/us-artists-article-functional-music.md)). A 2024 Harper's report claims Spotify fills jazz, ambient and lo-fi playlists with cheap "Perfect Fit Content" **(snippet)**. Spotify says all its music comes from licensed third parties.

Example (inference): if a stream from an Indonesian listener pays USD 0.001–0.003 and Netrilis passes on 75%, **Rp 30M a month needs about 0.8–2.5 million streams a month**. Lo-fi brands that do this, like Strange Fruits' three channels (~7 million Spotify streams a day in 2023), run large catalogues with label deals.

### YouTube

Added 2026-09-19. A music channel can earn in three ways: ads and Premium on its own videos, Content ID when other people's videos use its music, and fan funding (Super Chat, memberships).

**Getting into the Partner Program (YPP)**
- Today: 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days) for ads and Premium revenue. From 500 subscribers plus 3,000 watch hours, a channel gets fan funding and Shopping only **(snippet)**.
- **From 1 February 2027, new channels need 8,000 watch hours or 20M Shorts views.** Channels already in YPP are not affected. Shorts revenue will also be paid only to channels with 10M Shorts views in the last 90 days ([YouTube blog, 10 Aug 2026](../external_sources/web/blog.youtube/news-and-events-youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn.md)).
- So there are about 4.5 months left to qualify under the easier rule. Long music videos (1-hour mixes) add watch hours faster than short videos (inference).
- Premium: creators share 30% of net Premium revenue and 60% of Premium Lite revenue, split by watch time. Long-form videos get 55% of their share and Shorts 45%. Premium Lite is expanding to every Premium country (same source).

**The main risk: "inauthentic content"**
- YouTube will not pay channels whose videos are "mass-produced, generic, repetitive" or "made with a template", including "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates" ([YouTube monetization policies](../external_sources/web/support.google.com/youtube-answer-1311392-hl-en.md)). The policy was renamed from "repetitious" on 15 July 2025. YouTube says the rule itself is not new ([Music Ally](../external_sources/web/musically.com/2025-07-10-youtube-updates-mass-produced-and-repetitious-content-policy.md)).
- Reviewers look at the channel as a whole: its main theme, top and newest videos, and titles and thumbnails. Losing monetization affects the whole channel, not single videos.
- A channel of static-image AI lo-fi loops fits the banned description almost word for word.
- What reads as authentic (inference): visible human performance (playing, composing, studio process), a clear idea or story for each mix, and original visuals. Alip Ba Ta (fingerstyle guitar, 5.6M subscribers by 2022) was estimated at Rp 555M–8.8B a year. That is a wide SocialBlade-style estimate reported by [detik](../external_sources/web/hot.detik.com/music-d-5716524-youtuber-alip-ba-ta-diperkirakan-raup-rp-8-8-m-setahun.md), not a confirmed figure.

**What it pays**
- Music is a low-paying niche: the music CPM averages about USD 1.36 **(snippet)**. For Indonesian viewers, one estimate puts creator earnings at about **Rp 3,200 per 1,000 views** **(snippet)**.
- 24/7 livestreams usually show one ad when a viewer joins and none after, so long watch time earns little ([Water & Music](../external_sources/web/waterandmusic.com/the-economics-of-24-7-lo-fi-hip-hop-youtube-livestreams.md), 2020 article).
- The lo-fi brands there (Chillhop, College Music) earned mainly from **Spotify**, not YouTube. YouTube was their shop window. Estimates for Lofi Girl range from USD 20–45k a month to USD 3.5M a year **(snippet)**. They disagree, so neither is reliable.
- **Covers:** the owner of the song usually claims the video through Content ID and takes most of the ad money, often 50–75% **(snippet)**. Only original compositions keep the full share.

**Content ID on your own music**
- Individuals can't get Content ID directly; it comes through a distributor. Netrilis includes it ([Netrilis](../external_sources/web/netrilis.com/p-harga.md)).
- Content ID earns about USD 1 per 1,000 views on videos that use your music **(snippet)**.
- It conflicts with licensing: tracks sold to creators or cafés, or given away for exposure, will trigger claims on the buyers' own videos. Use an allowlist, or keep a separate catalogue that is not registered with Content ID.

**Example (inference)**
Rp 30M a month needs about **9M monthly views from Indonesian viewers** at Rp 3,200 per 1,000, or roughly **2–4M views from a global audience** at USD 0.5–1 per 1,000. Reaching a global audience means English titles ("lofi for study"), where competition is heaviest. An Indonesian-flavoured angle (keroncong lo-fi, gamelan ambient, dangdut koplo instrumentals) stands out more, but mostly draws low-paying Indonesian views.

**Tax**
- Google withholds US tax only on earnings from **US viewers**. The rate is 0–30% depending on treaty benefits claimed on the tax form in AdSense.
- Without a valid form, Google can withhold **24% of worldwide earnings** from an individual account ([Google](../external_sources/web/support.google.com/youtube-answer-10391362-hl-id.md)). The form must be renewed every three years; the yearly deadline is 10 December.
- Snippets say the Indonesia–US treaty with an NPWP gives 0–10%. The income is reported on the annual SPT, and US tax withheld is credited under PPh 24 **(snippet, not checked)**.

Sell instrumental tracks to video makers worldwide. Earnings are in USD and don't depend on Indonesian buyers.

|---|---|---|

| AudioJungle (Envato Market) | Non-exclusive 33%; exclusive 50–70% by sales level; USD 50 minimum payout | **(snippet)** |

This is a volume game: earnings build up over years from a large catalogue. AI-made tracks compete directly on generic "corporate", "upbeat" and "lo-fi" music.

### Background music for businesses (Indonesia)

The rules ([ANTARA](../external_sources/web/antaranews.com/berita-5024069-rincian-biaya-royalti-musik-untuk-kafe-restoran-serta-c-4d324f.md), [DJKI](../external_sources/web/dgip.go.id/artikel-detail-artikel-berita-mempelajari-kewajiban-bayar-royalti-untu-8a81cd.md), [LMKN FAQ](../external_sources/web/lmkn.id/faq.md)):
- Any business playing music to the public must pay royalties through LMKN: cafés, restaurants, shops, gyms, hotels, malls, transport. **A Spotify or YouTube Premium subscription does not cover it.**
- Restaurants and cafés: Rp 60,000 per seat per year for songwriters plus Rp 60,000 for related rights. A 20-seat café pays Rp 2.4M a year (≈ Rp 200k a month). Bars pay Rp 180,000 per m² for each right; nightclubs Rp 250,000 + 180,000 per m².
- Micro and small businesses can ask LMKN for a discount or exemption, based on size, visitors and how much music they use.
- Permenkum 27/2025 replaced the 2022 rules. It extends royalties to digital services, requires usage data every three months, and cuts LMKN's operating share from 20% to at most 8% **(snippet)**.

What happened in 2025:
- PT Mitra Bali Sukses (Mie Gacoan's licensee, 65 outlets) was taken to court for playing music without paying from 2022 to 2025 ([Tempo](../external_sources/web/tempo.co/hukum-polemik-royalti-lagu-para-musikus-bebaskan-karya-mereka-diputar-46cefd.md)).
- Some businesses switched to bird and water sounds, and PHRI NTB told members to stop playing music. LMKN answered that recorded nature sounds and instrumental music are covered too.
- Ahmad Dhani (Dewa 19), Radja, Juicy Luicy, Charly Van Houten and Thomas Ramdhan (GIGI) let businesses play their songs for free. Juicy Luicy's singer told cafés to play lo-fi or ambience instead.

The opportunity (inference):
- **DJKI names "working directly with independent musicians who give permission" as a legal alternative.** A composer who owns all rights and is **not** a member of an LMK can licence directly.
- A product could be a **café playlist subscription**: hours of original instrumental music (acoustic, lo-fi, light jazz, Indonesian styles like keroncong or gamelan-fusion) with a **written licence certificate per outlet**, priced below LMKN. For example Rp 50–100k a month against LMKN's Rp 200k for 20 seats. Chains with many outlets matter most.
- **Risks:**
  - It saves the café nothing if it also plays any other music, because it still owes LMKN.
  - LMKN's position on "all recordings" could lead to disputes, so every track must have clear rights with no samples and no uncleared AI material.
  - Musicians giving songs away for free and LMKN's small-business discounts cut into the price.
  - Get a lawyer's view before selling this (see [Open Questions](#open-questions)).

### Jingles and custom music

- Sribu and Fastwork list jingles **from about Rp 100,000**, delivered in 5–7 working days, with an instrumental (minus one) version or stems on request ([Sribu](../external_sources/web/sribu.com/id-music-audio-jingle-production.md)). Specialist studios (buatjingle.com, penciptalagu.id) sell jingles, anthems and marches for organisations; their prices were not checked.
- Small businesses need short audio for TikTok, Reels and store ads, and a custom jingle avoids copyright claims.
- It sells time, so income is capped by hours. It fits as a way to find first customers for the background-music product.

## Tax

- Royalties are taxable income. A DJP staff article says a local distributor paying a local musician withholds **PPh 23 at 15% of gross** ([DJP](../external_sources/web/pajak.go.id/en-node-86429.md), the author's personal opinion).
- Other secondary sources say the rate falls to an effective 6% for people earning under Rp 4.8B a year using the deemed-profit norm **(snippet)**. They conflict and were **not checked** against the regulations.
- Selling licences, jingles and playlist subscriptions directly is business turnover. An individual can use the **final 0.5% rate, with the first Rp 500M a year untaxed**, as in [Selling Ebooks](jual_ebook.md#tax).
- Foreign platforms (Epidemic, Envato, Pond5) pay from abroad. Tax treaty forms (W-8BEN for US platforms) and how income taxed abroad is credited in Indonesia were not researched.

## Fit With Our Goals (inference)

Measured against the [Rp 30M/month goal](../goals.md):

| Route | What Rp 30M/month takes | Capital | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Streaming (lo-fi/focus) | ~0.8–2.5M streams a month, a large catalogue and playlist placement | Low (Rp ~100k per release) | Long, uncertain; income is mostly foreign streams |
| YouTube channel | ~9M Indonesian views or ~2–4M global views a month; YPP entry before 1 Feb 2027 is easier | Low (camera, time) | Best as a shop window for the other routes; ad income alone is thin and the policy risk is high |
| Stock libraries | Hundreds of tracks, several years to build up | Low (time, gear) | Steady side income; under AI pressure |
| Café/business background-music licence | e.g. 300–600 outlets at Rp 50–100k a month | Low | **Best local fit**, but legally untested; see risks |
| Jingles | ~100+ jobs a month at Rp 300k, or fewer premium ones | Very low | Cash flow and first customers, doesn't scale |

- All routes need someone who can **compose and produce music** well. This research doesn't know whether we have that skill; it decides everything.
- Like ebooks, music needs no stock, COD or shipping, which avoids the problems in [Dropship on Shopee](dropship_shopee.md).
- The café/restaurant audience overlaps with the WhatsApp- and Facebook-reachable small-business owners in [Online Audience](online_audience.md).

## Suggested Approach

1. Check the legal basis first: ask a copyright lawyer, or DJKI/LMKN in writing, whether a café using only a non-LMK composer's licensed music owes LMKN anything.
2. Make 3–5 hours of original instrumental music with clean rights: no samples, no uncleared AI, a record of how each track was made.
3. Offer free 3-month licences to 10–20 cafés in Blitar/Malang to learn whether owners care, which styles they want and how they want to receive it (USB, streaming link, app).
4. At the same time, put the same catalogue on streaming through Netrilis and on AudioJungle/Pond5 non-exclusively. It costs little and shows which tracks people like.
5. Sell jingles to the same cafés for early cash.

- Details of LMKN's small-business discount or exemption: who qualifies and at what rate.
- Actual YouTube RPM for instrumental/lo-fi channels with mostly Indonesian vs global audiences (need real channel data, not calculators).
- Does YouTube treat long instrumental mixes with a performance clip or changing visuals as authentic? Look for channels that were demonetized under the July 2025 wording.
- Real per-stream pay for Indonesian listeners on Spotify and YouTube Music (the snippets give only global averages).
- Current official rates for Envato, Pond5 and Artlist contributors (help pages blocked).
- Whether Epidemic Sound or Artlist are signing Indonesian composers, and what they pay upfront.
- Correct PPh treatment of streaming royalties from Netrilis and of foreign platform payouts.
- Existing Indonesian competitors selling licensed background music to businesses (in-store music services such as Mood Media or local ones).
