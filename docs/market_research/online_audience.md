# Online Audience Behavior in Indonesia

Research done 2026-09-17 on how Indonesians use the internet: who is online, which platforms they use and for how long, how they discover and buy, how they pay, when they shop and what they distrust. The main sources are the APJII 2026 and 2025 surveys (face-to-face, 8,700 respondents aged 13+), DataReportal's Digital 2026 report (platform ad-tool data plus GWI survey data) and brand-side surveys (NielsenIQ, Totem, Jakpat, Populix, GoodStats). **APJII figures are read through news coverage (via news)**; the APJII report itself is behind a registration form. Figures seen only in search results, because the page was blocked or paywalled, are marked **(snippet)**. Most brand surveys are online panels, so they over-represent heavy internet users.

## Summary

- **Almost everyone who matters commercially is online.** 235.3M users, 81.72% of the population (APJII 2026, via news). Java is 85.95%; urban 84.75% vs rural 78.18%. People who are offline mostly lack a device (34%) or don't know how to use one (31.5%).
- **WhatsApp is the backbone.** About 9 in 10 internet users are active on WhatsApp each month, and 71% of adult internet users message a business account on it **every day** (Kantar for Meta, 2026, snippet). 95% of online-selling businesses sell through chat apps (BPS).
- **TikTok is the most-used app, but not for every age group.** It is the most frequently used social app in APJII 2026 (31.8%). Instagram skews Gen Z; Facebook stays strong from millennials up. TikTok's ad tools reach 180M adults (88.9% of adults), with a female skew (56.3%). Facebook's ad audience skews male (57.8%).
- **Social media runs the path to purchase.** Search engines are still the top brand-discovery source (38.3%), but social ads (37.3%) and social comments (32.6%) are close. Social networks are the main place people research brands (3 in 5).
- **Live shopping and affiliate links are mainstream.** 78% of online shoppers named live shopping their favourite Mega Sales feature, and 50% bought through affiliate links (NielsenIQ, 12.12 2024). 85% have watched or bought from a brand livestream, 70% of them on TikTok Shop (Totem 2025).
- **Deals decide.** Discounts and free shipping (70% each) drive mega-sale participation. Deal-seeking is the highest in Asia, and also the main reason people abandon influencer purchases (Totem).
- **Cash is not dead online.** 68.2% choose COD in online stores (APJII, via Databoks, snippet on year). E-wallets are the most-used payment method overall (62%, Populix).
- **Trust is fragile.** 66% of adults met a scam attempt in the past year and 35% were victims; direct messages and SMS were the most common channel (GASA 2025). APJII 2026 puts online fraud at 13.6% of users.
- **Timing:** evening (18:00–21:00) is the classic online-shopping prime time. Lunch hour (12:00) is the top hour in SIRCLO's brand data. Ramadan shifts activity to sahur (03:00–05:00) and before iftar (16:00–19:00).

### Region, place, gender, age and education (APJII 2026, via news)

- **Region:** Java 85.95% (58.24% of all users), Kalimantan 80.40%, Sumatra 78.24% (20.74% of users), Bali–Nusa Tenggara 78.14%, Sulawesi 72.58%, Maluku–Papua 69.74%.
- **Urban vs rural:** 84.75% vs 78.18%. Rural areas hold 39.41% of users, so rural buyers are a large online group, not a niche.
- **Gender:** men 83.95%, women 79.79%. The gap is narrowing.
- **Generation:** Millennials 90.34% and Gen Z 89.02% have the highest penetration.
- **Education:** university 92.49%, senior high 90.44%, junior high 82.48%, primary or none 74.84%.
- **Why the rest are offline:** no device 34%, don't know how 31.5%, data too expensive 17.2%, see no benefit 12.9% ([Bisnis](../external_sources/web/teknologi.bisnis.com/read-20260531-101-1977458-survei-apjii-2026-ungkap-alasan-1828-pendudu-6a2c58.md)).
- **Population context** (UN via DataReportal): median age 30.4; 60% urban. Ages 25–54 are 42.5% of the population, which is the main home-owning and household-buying group.

- TikTok is the top app for every generation (30.6–32.3%). In 2025 it was far more skewed: Gen Z 42.27%, Boomers 21.78%.

- Instagram falls with age: Gen Z 30.8%, Millennials 28.7%, Gen X 24.4%, Boomers 20.1%.
- In 2025, YouTube was the top app for Gen X (31.69%) and Boomers (39.11%). Instagram reached only 7.15% of Gen X and 4.44% of Boomers.
- **Short video** is the main type of entertainment for Gen Z (28.4%), Millennials (30.1%) and Gen X (30.5%).

## Time Spent and Why People Go Online

- **We Are Social/GWI:** 21h 50m a week on social media including online video, over 3 hours a day, spread over 7.7 platforms a month. TikTok and WhatsApp have about the same daily time per Android user (1h 53m and 1h 52m). YouTube has the longest sessions (16m 49s), followed by Snack Video (15m 04s).
- **APJII self-reported social media time:**
  - 2026: 1–2 h a day 44.9%, 2–3 h 37.9%, 3–4 h 6.9%, over 4 h 2.4%, under 1 h 7.9%.
  - 2025: 1–2 h 34.1%, 2–3 h 33%, 3–4 h 15%, over 4 h 14.8%.
  - The big drop in heavy use may also reflect a change in the question. The two sources measure differently: GWI includes video watching, APJII asks about social media only.
- **Reasons for using the internet (APJII 2026):**
  - communication and social networking 19.9%
  - entertainment (streaming, games) 19.7%
  - information and news 19.6%
  - e-commerce and services 18.7%
  - education and work 10.9%
  - financial services 5.8%
  - public services 4.5%
  - In 2025 the top uses were social media 24.80%, news 15.04% and online transactions 14.95% ([Antara 2025](../external_sources/web/antaranews.com/berita-5019229-apjii-catat-tingkat-penetrasi-internet-indonesia-capai-461987.md)).
- **Reasons for using social media (GWI):** staying in touch with family and friends, filling spare time, and finding inspiration for things to do and buy.
- **Jakpat H2 2025** (2,240 respondents): scrolling social media and YouTube are the top online activities, and most people use social media for entertainment and information ([Jakpat, preview only](../external_sources/web/insight.jakpat.net/indonesia-mobile-entertainment-social-media-trends-2nd-semester-of-2025.md)).
- **Streaming vs TV:** more than two-thirds watch streaming weekly, but linear TV still takes slightly more viewing time.

## Discovery and Research

- **Where people discover brands** (GWI via [Campaign Brief Asia](../external_sources/web/campaignbriefasia.com/2025-11-06-digital-2026-indonesia-reveals-social-media-user-identities-f192ae.md)):
  - search engines 38.3%
  - social media ads 37.3%
  - comments on social media 32.6%
- **About a fifth of internet users clicked a sponsored social post** in the past month.
- **Social networks are the main place people research brands**, used by 3 in 5. Google still leads search referrals, but its share fell 1.7% YoY.
- **After visiting a website,** the most common ways to interact with a brand online are watching its video and following it on social media.
- **More than a quarter watch influencer videos or vlogs** every week.
- **Influence is strongest in Asia** ([Totem 2025](../external_sources/web/talktototem.com/insights-reports-influencer-indonesia.md), 523 consumers): 27% rate influencers as high-impact at discovery (Asia 21%) and 25% at consideration and purchase.
  - Consumers prefer relatable micro-influencers, product demonstrations and community content.
  - Offline word of mouth still matters.
- **56.1% worry about telling real from fake** online.

## Social Commerce: Live Shopping and Affiliates

- **NielsenIQ, 12.12 Mega Sales 2024** ([NIQ](../external_sources/web/nielseniq.com/global-en-insights-analysis-2025-indonesia-mega-sales-5-key-consumer-i-e85f5e.md); 1,016 internet users aged 15+ in 41 cities, all income classes):
  - **Awareness:** 88% knew about Mega Sales. Free shipping and discounts (70% each) were the main incentives.
  - **Sales:** Rp 31.2T in the sale week, 6.6× an average day's sales.
  - **Categories bought:** fashion 62%, personal care 52%, food and drinks 43%.
  - **Live shopping** was the favourite feature (78%). 51% bought more often through lives and 44% spent more. The most trusted hosts are store owners (51%) and influencers (50%), and official stores are preferred.
  - **Affiliate links:** 50% bought through one, for discounts (76%), product quality (62%), price (58%), reviews (55%) and trust in the influencer (47%).
  - **Disclosure:** 84% trust an influencer more when a paid partnership is disclosed.
- **Totem 2025:**
  - **Livestreams:** 85% have watched or bought from a brand livestream, 70% of viewers on TikTok Shop, then Shopee Live.
  - **Ranking:** Indonesia is #2 of 7 Asian markets for social commerce enthusiasm, with growth especially in tier-3 cities.
  - **Buying and holding back:** product demos and deals drive purchases. Deal-seeking, doubts about quality and too many checkout steps hold them back.
- **GoodStats live shopping survey** (June 2024, 300 respondents aged 18–50; [GoodStats](../external_sources/web/goodstats.id/article-popularitas-melejit-bagaimana-kebiasaan-masyarakat-indonesia-s-007c78.md)):
  - **How often:** 48.7% buy through live shopping a few times a month, 22.7% a few times a week, 5% daily.
  - **Platforms used regularly:** TikTok Shop 56%, Shopee 33%.
  - **Why:** cheaper prices 49.8%, promos 17.1%, talking directly with the seller 15.1%.
  - **What they buy:** clothing and accessories 61.3%, electronics 33.3%, cosmetics and skincare 30%.
  - **When:** 18:00–22:00 (40%) or 22:00–02:00 (32%).
  - **How long:** 65% watch for under 30 minutes.
- **The buyer base is broad but careful with money:** 99M people bought consumer goods online, 67.6% of purchases on mobile; food is the biggest category, about USD 13B a year (GWI). Per-person online spend fell to Rp 470k a month in H1 2025 ([Jakpat via Tirto](../external_sources/web/tirto.id/tren-belanja-online-2025-fokus-kebutuhan-pokok-hftr.md)); see [Produk Rp 100–500 Ribu](produk_100rb_500rb.md#buying-power).

## Messaging and Chat Commerce

- **71% of adult internet users message a WhatsApp business account every day** (Kantar Business Messaging Usage for Meta, presented 11 Aug 2026; snippet from Kompas and Investortrust). The figure was 60% in 2024 and 66% in 2025. Business accounts here include online shops, brand customer service and local service providers.
- **Businesses sell through chat** (BPS Statistik E-Commerce 2023, via [GoodStats](../external_sources/web/goodstats.id/article-mayoritas-usaha-e-commerce-berjualan-lewat-aplikasi-pesan-instan-hmfph.md)):
  - chat apps 95.33% of online-selling businesses
  - social media 33.29%
  - email 3.34%
  - own website 1.77%; marketplace accounts are also rare
- **Implication:** the conversation, including questions, bargaining and order confirmation, happens in WhatsApp even when the ad or discovery happened on TikTok, Instagram or Facebook.

## Payment

- **Payment methods consumers use** (online and offline together; Populix Consumer Confidence Index 2025, 1,100 respondents, via [GoodStats](../external_sources/web/goodstats.id/article-bukan-qris-ini-metode-pembayaran-terfavorit-konsumen-indonesia-897862.md)):
  - e-wallet 62%
  - QRIS 54%
  - cash 51%
  - bank transfer 28%
  - debit card 12%
  - paylater and credit card 8%
  - Search summaries that give e-wallets 80% misquote this; the page says 62%.
- **ShopeePay is the most-used e-wallet** among Gen Z (77%) and millennials (71%) (Populix 2023). QRIS had 56M users in Q1 2025 (ASPI).
- **COD in online stores: 68.2%** chose cash on delivery (APJII, via [Databoks](../external_sources/web/databoks.katadata.co.id/en-technology-telecommunications-statistics-6a2a64fa23b14-cod-the-main-da7f7b.md); the page is paywalled beyond that line, and the link to the 2026 survey comes from a search snippet).
- **Businesses mostly take cash:** 75.19% of online-selling businesses accept cash or COD, 17.44% bank transfer, 5.91% e-wallet, 1.02% QRIS (BPS 2023, via GoodStats).
- **Paylater is growing fast** (OJK, via news); see [Produk Rp 100–500 Ribu](produk_100rb_500rb.md#buying-power).

## When People Shop

- **Daily rhythm:**
  - **Evening:** 18:00–21:00 is prime time (54%), then 21:00–24:00 (36%) and 12:00–15:00 (33%) (Jakpat, H2 2022, about 3,000 respondents; [GoodStats](../external_sources/web/goodstats.id/article-pukul-berapa-prime-time-masyarakat-indonesia-dalam-berbelanja-e8500b.md); older data).
  - **Lunch:** in SIRCLO's brand transaction data, **12:00 is the top hour**, and it doubles during Ramadan ([SIRCLO](../external_sources/web/sirclo.com/press-sirclo-ungkap-pola-belanja-konsumen-untuk-membantu-brand-memaksi-bec123.md)).
- **Ramadan** (SIRCLO, InMobi and Telkomsel data, via SIRCLO):
  - **Volume:** transactions +115% from 2 weeks before Ramadan to 2 weeks after Idulfitri 2025. Fashion grew 4×, travel nearly 3×, electronics, mom & baby and FMCG about 2×.
  - **Planning:** 74% of consumers planned to spend more than last year, and people plan purchases 2–4 weeks before Ramadan. Offers get more aggressive when THR bonuses are paid.
  - **Peaks:** sahur 03:00–05:00, lunch 11:00–14:00, and before and after iftar 16:00–19:00.
  - **Timing offers:** daily essentials work at sahur. Flash sales and live sessions work before iftar, when people buy on impulse. Gifts and family packs work in the last two weeks before Lebaran.
- **Mega sales** (11.11, 12.12): sales reach 6.6× a normal day (NIQ).

## Trust and Scams

- **GASA "State of Scams in Indonesia 2025"** (online survey of 1,000 adults, Feb–Mar 2025; [Media Indonesia](../external_sources/web/mediaindonesia.com/ekonomi-825964-66-persen-orang-dewasa-indonesia-alami-penipuan-online-956064.md)):
  - **Exposure:** 66% met a scam in the past year, about 55 exposures per person.
  - **Victims and losses:** 35% were victims and 14% lost money, Rp 49T in total, about Rp 1.7M per victim.
  - **Channel:** direct messages (chat apps, SMS) are the most common scam channel.
- **APJII 2026:** online fraud is the most common security incident (13.6%), then data theft, hacking and phishing (7.8%). In 2025 the figure was 22.12% (snippet). These face-to-face figures are much lower than GASA's online-panel results, and the definitions differ.
- **What builds trust:** official stores, store owners hosting live sessions, disclosed paid partnerships (NIQ), reviews (55% of affiliate buyers cite them) and COD.
- **The catch for WhatsApp sellers:** buyers live in WhatsApp, but scammers do too. A business chatting from an unknown number starts out distrusted.

## What This Means for Our Projects

These are inferences from the data above, not tested findings.

### Jasa tukang in Blitar ([research](jasa_tukang_blitar.md), [validation kit](blitar_validation.md))

- **WhatsApp-first is supported.** Nearly all internet users are on it, and 71% message businesses daily. Java penetration is 85.95% and rural 78.18%, so regency households can be reached too.
- **Reach homeowners through Facebook, then TikTok, not Instagram.**
  - Home-owning ages (Millennials, Gen X, Boomers) still use Facebook about as often as TikTok (~31% each, APJII 2026).
  - Instagram use drops with age.
  - Local Facebook groups, already seen as informal competition, are the natural place to start.
- **Local search still matters.** Search engines are the top discovery source (38.3%), so a Google Maps/Business listing with reviews is worth having. This is an inference; no data covers local-services search.
- **Trust has to be earned.** Scam exposure is high and comes mostly through chat. Use a verified WhatsApp Business profile, show real job photos and reviews, offer pay-after-work or staged payment and a warranty. This matches the escrow and warranty idea in [jasa tukang](jasa_tukang.md#product-options).
- **For migrant-worker (PMI) families,** the family member abroad is probably online and on WhatsApp, and may be the one paying. This is untested; add it to Test 2 of the validation kit.

### Selling Rp 100–500k products ([research](produk_100rb_500rb.md))

- **Plan around TikTok Shop live and affiliates** with demo content. Buyers compare deals, so plan for vouchers or free shipping and a short checkout.
- **Offer COD** despite the return risk (see the order-confirmation fix in the [sales funnel](../chat_summary/sales_funnel.md)).
- **Schedule lives in the evening** (18:00–22:00), post at lunch, and in Ramadan add sahur and pre-iftar slots. Start Ramadan campaigns 2–4 weeks early.
- **Tukang tools:** Facebook's audience skews male and 25–34, which matches the buyer. TikTok's audience skews female, which suits household goods.

## Caveats and Conflicts

- **APJII question changes:** the 2025→2026 shifts in YouTube use (23.8% → 1.6%) and heavy social media time (>4 h: 14.8% → 2.4%) are too large to be real behaviour change. Compare years with care.
- **Facebook 2026:** 20.4% (Bisnis) vs 29.4% (GNFI, snippet). 29.4% fits the totals better.
- **DataReportal's social media user count** only counts the biggest platform's ad reach (TikTok), so it equals TikTok's figure. Ad reach is not the same as active users, and Meta and TikTok revise these numbers often.
- **Penetration:** APJII (81.72%, early 2026, face-to-face) and DataReportal (80.5%, end 2025, modelled) roughly agree.
- **Scams:** GASA 35% victims vs APJII 13.6% online fraud. The first is an online panel with a broad definition, the second face-to-face.
- **Old or small samples:** prime-time data is from 2022, the GoodStats live shopping survey had 300 people, and Totem 523 from an online panel.
- **Blocked sources:** BPS tables and the Kompas articles on WhatsApp business use and APJII platform use were blocked; those figures are snippets or come from other outlets.
