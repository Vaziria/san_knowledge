# Produk Rp 100–500 Ribu dengan Permintaan Tinggi

Product research done 2026-09-17 on the premise: *which goods priced Rp 100,000–500,000 have high demand in Indonesia?* The assumed goal is to sell them online (Shopee, TikTok Shop/Tokopedia). Most figures come from Compas.co.id market data (via news and its own blog), Jakpat surveys and seller blogs. BPS and OJK figures quoted through news are marked **(via news)**. No source reports sales **by price band**, so the band fit below is inferred from category prices. The marketplace-crawl MCP was not connected for this pass, so listing-level sold counts have not been checked yet.

## Summary

- **Rp 100–500k is a big-ticket purchase online.** Average e-commerce spend was Rp 470,516 **per month** in H1 2025, down 13% YoY ([Jakpat via Tirto](../external_sources/web/tirto.id/tren-belanja-online-2025-fokus-kebutuhan-pokok-hftr.md)). One item in this band uses up much of a buyer's monthly online budget, so it needs a strong reason to buy: a need, a gift, a season or a campaign (11.11, 12.12, Ramadan).
- **Consumers are cutting back on "secondary" goods** (fashion, electronics) and buying cheaper imports when they do buy. They still spend on beauty, food and home care.
- **Best fits for the band:** local **perfume** (Rp 150–400k), local **running/sports shoes** (Rp 250–500k+) and **modest fashion** for Ramadan (Rp 100–300k). Beauty supplements, motorcycle accessories/helmets and small kitchen appliances come next.
- **Existing brands capture 92–96% of FMCG growth.** Thousands of new brands enter every semester, but few gain much ([Compas via Katadata](../external_sources/web/katadata.co.id/digital-e-commerce-6a882591bc6de-belanja-kebutuhan-harian-makin-pindah-676c12.md)). Perfume is the exception: two newcomers reached the top 10 in Q1 2026.
- **Margins are squeezed:** marketplace fees of about 10–13% on Shopee before ads, and 15–30% all-in on TikTok Shop.
- **Farm tools, tukang tools and household goods** (added on request): household goods have the strongest online demand (Home & Living 15% of Shopee GMV). Tukang tools fit the band through entry cordless and hand tools. Farm tools suit B2B or offline sales better; see [Farm Tools, Tukang Tools and Household Goods](#farm-tools-tukang-tools-and-household-goods).
- **Regulation:** direct cross-border sellers cannot sell imports under USD 100 FOB per unit (about Rp 1.6M), which shields this whole band from them. Cosmetics (including perfume) must be halal-certified after **17 Oct 2026**.

### Non-FMCG

- **No Compas-style value data was found** for fashion, shoes, electronics or home goods, so evidence here is thinner.
- **Video commerce, top categories by share** (Mordor teaser): fashion & accessories 28%, beauty 20%, phones & electronics 15%, home & tools 9%, health & baby 7%.
- **Electronics:** Magpie IQ says unit growth is moderate and average prices are rising as buyers trade up. Brands compete with a large no-brand segment.
- **Shoes:** 41.3% of consumers buy shoes online ([IndSight](../external_sources/web/indsight.id/blog-dominasi-sepatu-lokal-dan-pergeseran-minat-analisis-data-percakap-ebd0b8.md)).

### Buying power

- **Online spend is falling per person** while the number of buyers rises: Rp 543k → Rp 470k a month (H1 2024 → H1 2025; Jakpat, 2,283 respondents). Quick-commerce spend rose 36%.
- **Jakpat's H2 2025 report** says shoppers are "more selective … focus on value" ([preview only](../external_sources/web/insight.jakpat.net/indonesia-e-commerce-trends-2nd-semester-of-2025.md)).
- **Paylater stretches budgets:** 32.8M bank paylater accounts with Rp 30.71T outstanding, +33.5% YoY (June 2026), and finance-company BNPL at Rp 13.62T, +54.7% YoY (OJK, via Kontan/Kompas). Paylater mostly helps items at the top of this band.
- **Middle class is shrinking:** 57.33M (2019) → 46.7M people (2025) (BPS, via news; see [jasa_tukang.md](jasa_tukang.md#summary)).

## Candidate Products

Scored on fit with the Rp 100–500k band, demand evidence, competition and barriers. Not validated with sales counts yet.

### 1. Local perfume (EDP / extrait), Rp 150–400k

- **Demand:** perfume is a Rp 6.1T category (2025) growing 53%. Compas calls it fragrance becoming a daily need.
- **Band fit:** strong. Top local brands sell at about Rp 160–350k (HMNS, Saff & Co.).
- **Competition:** fragmented. On Shopee, 12,965 brands and ~1.98M listings; the #1 brand (Mykonos) has only 5.27% share. Local brands hold the top 5, and newcomers Ahmed Al Maghribi and Morris entered the top 10 in Q1 2026 ([Compas](../external_sources/web/compas.co.id/article-brand-parfum-terlaris-q1-2026-shopee.md)).
- **Barriers:** a BPOM cosmetic notification, plus **halal certification after 17 Oct 2026** ([BPJPH](../external_sources/web/bpjph.halal.go.id/detail-bpjph-produk-kosmetik-wajib-bersertifikat-halal-pada-oktober-2026.md)). A contract manufacturer (*maklon*) that already has both lowers this barrier.
- **Why it fits:** small, light and cheap to ship, with high margins and repeat buys. It sells through TikTok content and works as a gift.

### 2. Local running and sports shoes, Rp 250–500k

- **Demand:** running communities grew 5.8× (Strava 2025, via IndSight), and demand is shifting from lifestyle to performance shoes. The footwear industry was forecast to grow 12–17% in 2025.
- **Band fit:** good at the entry level. Ortuseight and Specs start at about Rp 300k, Compass sells at Rp 200–700k.
- **Competition:** Ortuseight makes 35.5% of top-10 running-shoe revenue in e-commerce and was Shopee's best-selling shoe (May 2025). Specs has the best sentiment (17:1 positive). Aza shows the risk: high buzz, mostly about quality complaints.
- **Barriers:** size runs and returns, stock depth and quality control. Local production (e.g. Bandung/Cibaduyut makers) is possible, but not researched yet.
- **Why it fits:** a clear functional need and a growing community. A niche angle could work: wide feet, kids, or budget trail shoes.

### 3. Modest fashion (gamis, mukena, koko, hijab sets), Rp 100–300k

- **Demand:** seen as Rp 300T a year domestically (Kemenperin estimate, via Kompas). Gamis and mukena were TikTok Shop best-sellers in Ramadan 2026.
- **Weakening:** Ramadan–Eid 2026 sales rose only about 50% over normal days, against up to 200% in earlier years. Chinese imports win on price, and domestic fabric is more expensive because of VAT ([Kompas](../external_sources/web/kompas.id/artikel-en-potensi-pasar-dan-produksi-besar-modest-fashion-tertekan-bi-0099e8.md)).
- **Band fit:** good.
- **Barriers:** very seasonal, with inventory risk after Lebaran. Shopee charges its top 10% fee on women's Muslim clothing. Kompas quotes Kemenperin saying halal certification "will begin to be applied to Muslim clothing" from Oct 2026; **this needs checking**, because PP 42/2024 names mainly animal-derived materials such as leather.
- **Timing:** production would have to start by about Nov–Dec 2026 for Ramadan 2027 (expected around early Feb 2027).

## Farm Tools, Tukang Tools and Household Goods

Added 2026-09-17 on request. These three categories have **no Compas-style e-commerce sales data**, so evidence is about buyers and prices. Listing sold counts are still needed before any decision.

### Alat pertanian (farm tools)

- **Buyers:** 25.1M farming households (*rumah tangga usaha pertanian*), 28.19M farmers. Crops 15.6M households, livestock 12.0M, plantation (*perkebunan*) 10.9M (BPS Sensus Pertanian 2023, via news). East Java alone has 5.5M ([InfoPublik](../external_sources/web/infopublik.id/kategori-nusantara-804692-sensus-pertanian-2023-catat-rumah-tangga-usa-ba318f.md)).
- **Few farmers are online:**
  - 46.84% (13.12M) use modern farm machinery **or** digital technology. BPS counts internet or smartphone use as digital technology.
  - Only 21.93% of farmers are aged 19–39.
  - Among millennial farmers, 57.8% do not use digital technology ([BPS via Antara](../external_sources/web/antaranews.com/berita-3854265-bps-4684-persen-petani-pakai-alsintan-modern-dan-teknol-727b7e.md); millennial figure via Databoks).
  - Most farmers still buy at local farm-supply kiosks (*kios tani*). No data on online purchase share was found.
- **Buying power is good right now:**
  - The farmer terms-of-trade index (NTP) reached 129.19 in Aug 2026, reported as a record high.
  - Plantation farmers are strongest at 168.77, lifted by palm oil and rubber prices. Food crops are at 118.04, and horticulture is falling (124.47, −2.84% in a month).
  - Jan–Aug 2026 is 3.18% above a year earlier (BPS, via [Viva](../external_sources/web/viva.co.id/bisnis-1925536-naik-105-persen-bps-nilai-tukar-petani-di-agustus-berad-2bc57b.md)).
- **Products in the band:**
  - Manual knapsack sprayers; battery sprayers at the low end. Branded 16 L battery sprayers are listed at about Rp 525–625k, just above the band; cheaper models not checked.
  - Palm harvesting tools (*dodos*, *egrek*), pruning shears, hoes, small water pumps.
  - Electric grass trimmers: Tekiro RGT350 is Rp 512k, Black+Decker GL 260 Rp 550k (Blibli listings).
- **For:**
  - Plantation smallholders have cash now.
  - Tools wear out and get replaced every season.
  - Buyers are few and spread out, so competition is weaker than in consumer categories.
- **Against:**
  - Buyers are older and use online shopping less.
  - Deliveries go to rural areas, which costs more and takes longer.
  - Demand varies by crop and region.
  - It sells best through trust in the local kiosk. That suggests selling B2B to kiosks, or through agents or farmer groups, rather than B2C on marketplaces.

### Alat tukang (tukang tools)

- **Buyers:**
  - 8.7M construction workers (BPS Feb 2025, via news; see [jasa_tukang.md](jasa_tukang.md#market-size-and-demand/supply)).
  - Homeowners doing DIY.
  - Small workshops.
- **Market size (unreliable):** one report puts power tools at USD 352.6M (2024) growing ~5.2% a year, with Bosch, Makita, DeWalt and Hilti leading and Krisbow as the leading local brand. The same page also says "USD 4.9B by 2025", so its numbers contradict each other ([SourceReady](../external_sources/web/sourceready.com/report-detail-indonesia-power-tools-market-report-2025.md)).
- **Band fit:**
  - Entry-level 12V cordless drills sell from under Rp 200k on promo (JLD). Other value brands are Xenon, NRT-PRO, Ryu, Modern, Deli, Ingco and Kova ([emakbapak.id](../external_sources/web/emakbapak.id/bor-baterai-murah-berkualitas-shopee.md)).
  - "Pro" 20V cordless drills (Ryu RCI20V, Kova) start around Rp 900k, above the band.
  - So Rp 100–500k covers entry cordless drills, corded grinders and drills, hand-tool sets, and measuring tools (laser levels, meters).
- **For:**
  - Tools are consumed and replaced (grinding discs, drill bits, batteries).
  - Buyers are mostly men who shop on specs and price, which fits TikTok demo content.
  - The band fits both a tukang's own tools and DIY homeowners.
- **Against:**
  - Crowded with cheap Chinese no-brand and value brands.
  - Modern chains (AZKO, Mitra10, MR.D.I.Y.) are growing: 38.4% of home-improvement retail in 2025 ([Ken Research teaser](../external_sources/web/kenresearch.com/indonesia-home-improvement-market.md#market-breakdown)).
  - Warranty and returns are costly for battery tools.
  - It is **not verified whether power tools need mandatory SNI**. No regulation was found; check before importing.

### Rumah tangga (household goods)

- **Demand:**
  - **Home & Living** was joint-largest on Shopee Indonesia in Q1 2025 with Health & Beauty, each 15% of GMV and +18.2% YoY (Momentum Works, via a Statista snippet; not saved).
  - **Homecare FMCG** grew fastest at +78.3% in H1 2026, but its items are mostly low-ticket.
- **Products in the band:**
  - **Kitchen:** rice cookers, blenders, choppers; cookware sets (pots, pans); food containers.
  - **Around the house:** fans, irons, spin mops, storage and organisers, bedding.
- **Regulation (a barrier for others, an advantage if you comply):**
  - **Household electronics** must carry SNI, including irons, fans, blenders and rice cookers (Permenperin 7/2025; [izin.co.id](../external_sources/web/izin.co.id/blog-barang-elektronik-yang-wajib-sni.md)).
  - **Cookware and stainless flatware** need SNI from April 2025 (Permenperin 53/2024). A foreign factory must appoint an Indonesian representative as importer, with its own brand registered at DJKI ([PT PBS](../external_sources/web/ptpbs.co.id/post-permenperin-no-53-2024-tentang-sni-wajib-peralatan-masak-atau-per-fb7b64.md)).
  - **Stainless compartment food containers** need SNI from Permenperin 1/2026 (secondary source).
- **For:**
  - Largest non-FMCG category, bought by almost every household.
  - Moving house, marriage and Ramadan/Lebaran all create demand, and many items work as gifts.
  - SNI rules keep out casual importers, which leaves room for local brands or white-label goods with SNI.
- **Against:**
  - Well-known brands (Miyako, Cosmos, Philips, Maspion) dominate the appliance band.
  - Bulky items cost more to ship.
  - Unbranded goods risk takedowns for non-SNI electronics and cookware.
- **Best angle:** non-electric goods with no SNI requirement (storage, organisers, cleaning tools, bedding) cost the least to enter. Electric appliances and cookware make sense only with an SNI-holding manufacturer or brand partner.

### Comparison

| | Alat pertanian | Alat tukang | Rumah tangga |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyers | 25.1M farming households, older, less online | 8.7M construction workers + DIY | ~75M households |
| Online demand evidence | Weak (listings only) | Medium (many value brands on Shopee) | **Strong** (15% of Shopee GMV) |
| Fit with Rp 100–500k | Good (manual tools, low-end sprayers) | Good (entry cordless, corded tools, hand tools) | Good |
| Competition | Local kiosks, few online specialists | Chinese value brands, modern chains | Big appliance brands; fragmented non-electric |
| Regulation | Low for hand tools | Power-tool SNI not verified | SNI for appliances and cookware |
| Best channel | B2B to kiosks, farmer groups, WhatsApp | TikTok/Shopee demos | Shopee (Home & Living), TikTok |

- **Pick:**
  - **Rumah tangga (non-electric)** has the strongest online demand and the lowest barriers.
  - **Alat tukang** is second. It also fits the existing [jasa tukang research](jasa_tukang.md): the same tukang could be reached as both service providers and tool buyers.
  - **Alat pertanian** looks better as an offline or B2B distribution business than as a marketplace shop, except in plantation areas while palm oil and rubber prices stay high.

## Seller Economics and Rules

### Marketplace fees (2026, seller blog)

| Platform | Commission | Effective cost incl. ads |
|---|---|---|
| Shopee (non-Mall) | 10% for fashion/FMCG/lifestyle, 9–9.5% skincare/some electronics; +Rp 1,250 per order; +3% pre-order | 15–25% |
| Tokopedia / TikTok Shop | 6.97–10% (10% rate discounted to 8%) + dynamic commission; +Rp 1,250 per order | Tokopedia 12–20%; TikTok Shop 15–30% with affiliates (5–20%) |
| Lazada | 1.5–6%, capped at Rp 20k per item | 8–15% |

- Sources: [webekspor.com](../external_sources/web/webekspor.com/blog-biaya-admin-marketplace-indonesia-2026-shopee-tokopedia-lazada-da-9ea309.md). Another source says TikTok's per-item commission cap rose from Rp 40k to Rp 650k in mid-2026; this conflicts with webekspor and needs checking in Seller Center.
- **Example:** a Rp 200k product loses about Rp 30–60k to platform, ads and affiliates. The cost of goods should be under about 40% of the price to keep a healthy margin.

### Import rules

- **Cross-border floor:** platforms must not let foreign merchants sell finished goods directly to Indonesia below **USD 100 FOB per unit** (Permendag 31/2023, [CNBC](../external_sources/web/cnbcindonesia.com/news-20230927173658-4-476138-resmi-ecommerce-haram-jual-barang-impor-k-519610.md)). This does not stop imports brought in by local importers.
- **Import licences:** Permendag 8/2024 dropped the extra technical approval (*pertek*) for electronics, footwear, clothing and accessories that Permendag 36/2023 had added. Cosmetics and household goods need only a surveyor report ([Antara](../external_sources/web/antaranews.com/berita-4109298-pemerintah-terbitkan-permendag-8-2024-soal-larangan-bat-a8ee00.md)).
- **Halal:** cosmetics, drugs, chemical products and some *barang gunaan* must be certified after 17 Oct 2026 (PP 42/2024). Food was covered from Oct 2024.

## Recommendation

- **Shortlist:** (1) local perfume and (2) local running shoes.
  - Both match the band well and have documented demand growth.
  - In both, local brands lead, and in perfume newcomers can still break in.
- **Among farm, tukang and household goods:** non-electric household goods first, then entry-level tukang tools. Electric appliances and cookware need an SNI-holding supplier.
- **Modest fashion is a seasonal side line only:** worth testing for Ramadan 2027 with small batches, not as the core.
- **Before committing, validate with data:**
  1. Pull Shopee/Tokopedia listings with the marketplace-crawl MCP for keywords (*parfum edp*, *extrait de parfum*, *sepatu lari*, *gamis*, *mukena*, *bor baterai*, *rak organizer*, *sprayer*). Record price, sold count and rating, then count the share of sales in Rp 100–500k.
  2. Get quotes from 2–3 perfume *maklon* (MOQ, unit cost, BPOM and halal support) and 2–3 shoe makers.
  3. Build a unit-economics sheet per platform using the fees above.
