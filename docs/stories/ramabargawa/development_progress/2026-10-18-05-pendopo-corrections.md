## Done

- **Inner tumpang sari (uleng)**: each tier is now a full frame. The outer band steps outward and carries the rafters, as before. The **inner band steps inward**, so looking up shows the inverted stepped-pyramid ceiling, each step with a carved lip. A board closes the top, and the **nanasan** hangs from its centre.
- **Removed the "dhadha peksi" beam** across the top. The term came from memory and no source confirmed it.
- **Brunjung hip rafters (dudur)**, from the four corners to the ridge's tips.
- **Battens (reng)** across the rafters under the thatch, on every face.
- **Sunduk through the soko guru**, sticking out past them, with a **kili** peg at each end.
- **Padma umpak** under the soko guru: a square foot, two staggered rows of eight petals, a neck, a seat.
- **Every umpak in plain stone**: the block-masonry texture made them look like heaps of rubble, and an umpak is a single stone.
