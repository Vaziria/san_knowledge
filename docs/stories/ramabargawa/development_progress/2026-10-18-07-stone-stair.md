# 2026-10-18: The pendopo's stair, in chipped stone

The pendopo's steps were separated from the plinth and rebuilt from stylized stone blocks, after the modular stone video in `project/bargawa/references/`.

## Decision (the user's words)

| Decision | The user said |
|---|---|
| Separate the stair; stone modelling like the video | "separate pendopo stair, and make it stone modelling like reference that extracted from video" |

"Separate" was taken as its own object and collection inside `pendopo.blend`. Its own `.blend`, as a reusable piece, is offered if wanted.

## Done

- **`scripts/common/stone.py`**, a stone-block generator, the start of a modular stone kit:
  - a rounded box whose rounding wanders along the edges
  - an uneven surface from smooth noise
  - a slight taper and tilt
  - scooped chips at edges and corners (a plane cut, deepest in the middle of its span)
  - a **`wear`** value per point: 1 on rounded edges and chip faces
- **`stone.stair()`** lays each step as a row of 3–4 blocks of different lengths with 1.4 cm joints. Same widths and step heights as before (3.4 / 3.2 / 3.0 m wide, tops at 0.15 / 0.30 / 0.45 m).
- **Pendopo_Stair** is its own object, in a child collection `Pendopo_Stair` of `Pendopo`. The hermitage links `Pendopo`, so the stair comes with it (checked in a still).
- **Material `M_stone_kit`**: the `rock_surface` texture at a 1.4 m tile, plus a lighter tint from the `wear` attribute (Attribute › Multiply 0.45 › Mix Color toward #CECAC0): the worn-edge look of the video's texturing.
- **The mesh builder**:
  - `mesh.Parts` can carry a value per point, written as a float attribute.
  - `build(sharp=…)` keeps edges sharper than an angle hard under smooth shading.
- **Guide 01** gained Part 1b: the stone stair by hand, with modifiers (Bevel, chips, Shade Auto Smooth) or by sculpting like the video, plus the worn edges.

## Checks

Review stills `renders/checks/pendopo/` (`stair`, `stair_close`, and the new `front` view):

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Blocks looked like bars of soap: an even rounding, chips hidden inside it | Rounding varies along each edge; chips 3–8 cm deep and longer |
| Every edge chalk-white | Wear tint 0.6 → 0.45 |
| Chip borders looked melted | Sharp edges from 30° instead of 38° |

`pendopo.blend` was rebuilt with `-Force` (the user's open window needs File > Revert).
