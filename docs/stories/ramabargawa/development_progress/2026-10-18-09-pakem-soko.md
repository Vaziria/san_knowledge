## Found

- **The Kawruh Kalang's rules**, via Prijotomo 2005 (*Dimensi*):
  - everything is measured from the blandar–pengeret frame
  - the petungan Sri–Kitri–Gana–Liyu–Pokah: a joglo's beams must land on Kitri
  - soko guru length = the frame's diagonal
  - **umpak height = the post's perimeter** (soko guru), **half of it** (soko penanggap), lower still (soko emper)
  - the sunduk, molo and dudur sizes from the frame
  - gonja and santen
- **A real Joglo Lawakan, the Pendopo Agung Tamansiswa** (Yogyakarta, 1938; the Jogjacagar register):
  - soko guru 22 cm × 6.25 m on 80 cm umpak
  - soko penanggap 15 cm × 3.75 m on 30 cm umpak
  - soko emper 10 cm × 2.65 m on 16 cm umpak
  - spacing 6 × 5 m and 5 m; the floor 60 cm up
- **The umpak rule checks out on Tamansiswa** (88 cm against 80, and 30 against 30). The soko guru length rule does not, literally.
- Measured joglo proportions (Novianto et al., ARDC 2025) and sellers' sizes, as weaker guides.

## Our pendopo against it

- **The plan proportions and the 0.6 m floor match.**
- **The posts are about twice as thick for their height** (1 : 12 against 1 : 25–28).
- The soko guru are relatively short (1.33 against 1.67 times the soko penanggap).
- The umpak are far lower than the pakem.
