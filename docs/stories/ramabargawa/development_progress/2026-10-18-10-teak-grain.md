## What was wrong

Close-ups (`renders/checks/pendopo/soko_wood.png`, `beam_wood.png`, new views) showed three problems:
- **The wrong texture**: `wood_table_worn` is a polished tabletop with swirly figure, reading as dark, blotchy leather.
- **Box projection doesn't know a piece's direction**, so there was no grain along the posts or beams.
- Every piece looked the same, and nearly black in shade.

## Done

- **Texture**: teak is now Poly Haven **`teak_veneer`** (real teak, straight grain; its grain runs horizontally in the image). Tile 1.2 m; palette mix 0.25 (teak) and 0.5 (teak_dark).
- **Grain along every piece** (`mesh.py`, `_grain_uvs`):
  - Any piece in a timber material (`GRAIN_MATS`: M_teak, M_teak_dark) is split into its separate pieces.
  - Each piece's long axis is found from its points.
  - Each face gets **UVs**: U along the length, V across the face, in metres. That works like a per-piece unwrap.
  - Works for posts, beams, tumpang sari, rafters, kili, doors and planks, in the pendopo and in the huts, pawon and pen.
- **No two pieces alike**: a random UV offset per piece, and a **`tone`** attribute per piece that sets a Hue/Saturation/Value brightness of 0.82–1.18 in the material.
- **Material**: teak uses Texture Coordinate UV with Flat projection (`polyhaven.UV_KEYS`); everything else keeps box projection.
- **Guide 02**: a new "Wood" section (UVs by hand: Cube Projection, rotate the islands along the grain, offset per piece; the tone node), the teak rows updated, two new pitfalls.

## Then: the wood reference's layers

The user asked: "is wood using reference video-wood-plank-material-javad.md", then "why dont use that ?". It was not used: "make it correct" had been read as only the technical fix. That was a mistake, since the reference was given for exactly this. So the reference's layer stack was built in nodes on all teak (`textures.py`, `weathered_wood`):
1. Gradient re-colour through a ramp of teak browns (mix 0.55).
2. Fibres: noise stretched along U (the grain), darkening.
3. **Worn lighter edges**: a second UV layer **`EdgeUV`** from the builder (x 0..1 across each face, y its width) gives the distance to a face's long edges in the shader. Edges lighten within about 2 cm, broken up by noise, mix 0.5 (0.7 looked like painted lines).
4. **Dirt from the foot**: object Z from 2.0 m (none) down to 0.9 m (full), blotchy, mix 0.6.
5. Damp streaks: noise stretched along Z, darkening.
6. Final balance: saturation 0.9.
7. Roughness pass: 0.5–0.78, rougher at edges and dirt.

Checked in `beam_wood`, `soko_wood` and `umpak_pen` (pendopo) and `hut_elders` (the hut posts darker at the foot). Both files rebuilt with `-Force`. Guide 02 gained "Weathered teak", every layer by hand, and the reference doc records that it is used.
