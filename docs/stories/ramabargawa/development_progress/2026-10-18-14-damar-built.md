## Done

- **The model**: `scripts/common/damar.py` (pure geometry) and `scripts/objects/damar.py`, built into `blender/objects/damar.blend` with `run.ps1 object damar`:
  - `Damar` (an asset): `Damar_Stand` (turned teak foot and shaft), `Damar_Lamp` (the bronze head, oil, wicks), and `Damar_Flame_1..4`.
    - The head: socket, three-tier plinth, four leaf dishes on the diagonals with curled flanges and struts, and the ascetic on a lotus cushion holding a lontar, with a flame aureole behind him.
    - `Damar_Flame_1..4` are four instances of the flame piece, all lit, as in S1.
  - `Damar_Flame` (an asset): one flame and a 0.5 W warm point light. A light state can put it on any wick (`damar.FLAMES`): one flame for S4.
  - 2,763 faces. Height 0.89 m; the flames at about 0.8 m, the eye level of people sitting on mats.
- **Materials**:
  - `textures.bronze`: olive patina in the hollows (Ambient Occlusion) and in faint patches; polished where the `wear` attribute marks rubbed places (rims, the socket lip, the ascetic's knees and crown).
  - `teak_turned`: grain UVs, without the weathering layers.
  - `mesh.flame_material`: emission graded from gold to orange.
  - New palette entries: bronze, oil, wick.
- **In the hermitage**: `dressing.py` links `Damar` at (0, 1.3) on the pendopo's floor, inside the soko guru. The placeholder lamp is gone.
- **Code**:
  - `geo.lathe` (turned shapes)
  - `mesh.point_light`
  - `textures._kit`: the node helpers, now shared by the weathered wood and the bronze
  - `polyhaven.CARED_KEYS`
  - `views.py`: `DAMAR_VIEWS`; night stills (`{"night": True}`: the sun off, the sky down); clip start scaled for close-ups; the place's `lamp` and `lamp_night`
- **Guide [07-modelling-damar.md](../../../../project/bargawa/references/07-modelling-damar.md)**: the lamp by hand with the same numbers (Screw modifier, bevelled curves, Proportional Editing, Solidify, Simple Deform, the flame and its light as a collection instance, the bronze node recipe).

## Checks

Stills in `renders/checks/damar/` (`lamp`, `head`, `ascetic`, `dish`, `top`, `night`, `night_head`) and `renders/checks/jatisrana/` (`lamp`, `lamp_night`). Fixed along the way:
1. **Night was black**: the flame mesh, around its own light, shadowed it. The flame no longer casts shadows.
2. **The flames read white**: emission 12, then 3.5, were too bright. At 1.4 they show gold to orange.
3. **The head glared copper-pink by day**: the lights were 1.5 W a centimetre away. Now 0.5 W with a 2 cm radius.
4. **The leaf flanges were flat 5-point diamonds**: now curved strips.
5. **The arms and struts read as flat bands**: their tubes had 6–8 sides under 40° sharp shading. Now 12.
6. **The aureole read as a stepped tower**: its tongues pointed straight out. They curl up now.
7. **The figure was a stack of spheres**: now one lofted torso with a thin waist and sloping shoulders.

In the hermitage, the lamp stands between the sons' mats and Jamadagni's bale, at the right scale beside the seated figure. At night it is one warm point in the dark.

`damar.blend` and `jatisrana.blend` were built; `pendopo.blend` was not touched this time.
