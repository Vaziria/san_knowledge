# 2026-10-21: The user's soil on Jatisrana's bare ground

## Request (the user's words)

| Request | The user said |
|---|---|
| Use their soil material | "i have soil material on opened blender, this is better for jatisrana" |

The material is `Soil` in `project/bargawa/blender/textures/material_manual.blend` (open in the user's Blender, saved 21:03). It is the same kind of setup as their spiky ground, so it took the spiky ground's place: the yard round the pendopo and the huts, and the far bank round Citrarata's spot.

## What their material is

- The photo `blender/assets/photos/1cb0165c04eb35eb7bd78df3f83cc534.jpg`: soil with leaf litter, 1024 × 1024, seamless (the edge pixels differ no more than neighbours do), once over a 5.59 m plane.
- The photo → Base Color, and → Bump (Strength 1.0, Distance 0.8).
- RGB to BW → Color Ramp (black 0.655, white 0.932) → Displacement (Scale 0.1, Midlevel 0), Displacement and Bump, on a Simple subdivision 6 (8.7 cm).
- Metallic 0.177, Roughness 0.445, IOR 1.0.

## Done

- **The library** (`run.ps1 texture ground`): `M_ground_soil` and `GN_ground_soil` in `blender/textures/ground.blend`, beside the spiky ground (kept), with a sample patch.
  - The photo repeats every 5.6 m, with the user's bump from the photo.
  - The relief group carries the user's ramp.
  - Shading is matte (Roughness 0.85, Metallic 0, IOR 1.5), as the user chose for the thatch ("Realistic", note 08).
- **Jatisrana** (`run.ps1 place jatisrana`): the patches are now `BareGround_Yard` (14,755 faces, 118,040 triangles) and `BareGround_FarBank` (2,920 faces, 23,360 triangles), in the soil. Same places, same fades as before.
- **Code**:
  - `common/terrain.py`:
    - The two looks are `SPIKY` and `SOIL` in `RELIEF_GROUNDS`.
    - `BARE` picks the one Jatisrana wears.
    - `relief_ground_material`, `relief_patch`, `bare_ground` and `relief_sample` replace the spiky-only functions.
    - The modifier is named `Relief`.
  - `common/jatisrana.py`: `SPIKY_FADE` is now `BARE_FADE`.
  - `common/textures.py`: `ground_soil` in the ground family.
  - `textures/build.py`: a sample for every relief ground.
  - `places/jatisrana.py`.
  - `game/jatisrana.py`: the rule is now `BareGround`.

## Checks

- **The same as the user's**: their plane rendered with their `Soil` and with the library's `M_ground_soil` (with the user's shading set to matte): the same mean colour, (33.2, 30.2, 25.8) both. With their own shading it is darker and browner, (23.5, 19.9, 14.6).
- **Their displacement does nothing**:
  - Their plane renders the same with Displacement and Bump as with Bump only (no pixel differs by more than 2/255).
  - The ramp's black stop, 0.655, is on linear brightness. Only 0.03 % of the photo's pixels are that bright (the brightest is 0.817), and none of the plane's 4,225 points lands on one.
  - The library's sample is just as flat: no point raised.
  - So the look is the photo and its strong bump. The Geometry Nodes relief is kept, at the same triangle count as the spiky ground had, so a lower ramp later only needs a rebuild.
- **Stills** `renders/checks/jatisrana/` (`pendapa`, `huts`, `yard`, `far_bank`): fine soil in the yard and on the far bank, fading into the ground. Under the sky it reads greyer and a little lighter than on the user's plane.

## Open questions

- **Relief or not**: lower the ramp's black stop (about 0.3) so the light leaves and pebbles rise, or keep it flat as on the user's plane?
- **Shading**: matte, as here, or the user's own Metallic 0.177, Roughness 0.445, IOR 1.0 (darker and browner)?
- **Close up**: the strong bump (Distance 0.8) shows the photo's pixels as small blocks within about 2 m. A Cubic interpolation on the image or a smaller distance would soften it, if a shot comes that close.
- **The photo's licence**: from the web with a hashed name, like the spiky one. Check it before anything is published.
- The game's copy predates the patches (note 07); the soil's photo isn't in `game.game_material` yet. Left for when the game comes back.
