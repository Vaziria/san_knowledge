# san_remote

Runs JavaScript in your own browser through the San Remote extension, from a
CLI and from Claude over MCP. Through it, it schedules Threads posts;
uploads, lists, deletes and downloads Vidara videos; posts to Facebook
(your profile, a Page or a group), schedules, and lists or deletes profile
posts; and posts, schedules, moves and deletes threads on X. It writes
markdown articles into Medium and LinkedIn drafts and schedules them,
schedules TikTok videos through TikTok Studio, uploads YouTube videos and
Shorts through YouTube Studio, schedules Instagram photos, carousels and
Reels in Meta Business Suite or shares them at once on instagram.com, makes and deletes Telegram bots (through
@BotFather) and groups and adds and removes group members in Telegram Web,
and asks ChatGPT and Gemini
in a Temporary chat, and Claude on claude.ai in an incognito chat. `san_remote api` offers these site services to other
programs over Connect (gRPC), first the tower's workflows.
