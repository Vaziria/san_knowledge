# WILWATIKTA (Majapahit) Online Museum

A general reference for the game's objects, given by the user on 2026-10-20: "this web can be object reference to our game". **Address**: https://wilwatiktamuseum.wordpress.com/

**What it is**: a WordPress blog run by Deddy Endarto, from 2011 to 2014, in Indonesian. Wilwatikta is the Majapahit kingdom's official name in its inscriptions. It shows artefacts of Majapahit and the wider archipelago: statues, temples and gates, jewellery, crowns, heirloom weapons, household and ritual objects, inscriptions and dance. Most entries give the object's period, and many give its size. Several are copied from museum or auction records, which the posts name (the Metropolitan Museum, the Asian Art Museum San Francisco, auction houses).

**Cached in San Knowledge** (`docs/external_sources/web/wilwatiktamuseum.wordpress.com/`):
