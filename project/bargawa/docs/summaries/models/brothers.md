# C06 The four brothers

Part of the [model design](index.md). Look and traits: [models.md, C06](../../../../../docs/stories/ramabargawa/models.md).

## In Chapter 1

- **Scene 1**: on the mats with Bargawa. Rumawan tells him their father is waiting.
- **Scene 3**: each refuses the order in his own way, and is cursed:

| Brother | Trait (Rickyansyah 2019) | His refusal |
|---|---|---|
| Rumawan | eldest, firm, polite, responsible | a sembah and a quiet answer |
| Susena | ambitious, rebellious, emotional | stands up in anger |
| Wasu | the middle one | weeps |
| Wiswawasu | proud, indifferent | turns his face away |

- **Scene 4**: they are back, sitting, and do not remember.

## Build

- **One base**: a hermit's son made with MPFB, with a plain white or undyed kain and a gelung, and no jewellery. Heights 1.68–1.74 m, all shorter than Ramabargawa (1.80 m).
- **Four variants** of it, by:
  - the face (MPFB face settings)
  - the age (Rumawan about 30, Wiswawasu about 22)
  - the hair (gelung shape)
  - the colour of the cloth (white, undyed, grey, a faded ochre)
- **The curse**: one material setting turns each brother to grey stone, desaturated and rough. He holds his pose until S4.

## Animations

Sila sitting with idle breathing, speech, the four refusals above, standing up.

## Budget

About 10k triangles each. The four share one 1K costume set; each has his own 1K face texture.
