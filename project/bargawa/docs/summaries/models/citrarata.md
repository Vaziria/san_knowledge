# C07 Prabu Citrarata

Part of the [model design](index.md). Look and costume: [models.md, C07](../../../../../docs/stories/ramabargawa/models.md).

## In Chapter 1

- **Scene 2 only**, and only from across the lake.
- He stands on the far bank (`CITRARATA_SPOT` in `scripts/common/jatisrana.py`, about 27 m from the bathing stone), against the low sun.
- He is seen as a dark shape with a bright edge. His face is never seen, and his singing is heard before he is.

## Build

- **MPFB body**: male, young, slim and handsome, 1.74 m.
- **Only what reads in silhouette and in the rim light**:
  - the crown (makutha) and sumping
  - a kalung, and the kelat bahu
  - a rich kain with gold, which catches the light
  - the keris at his back
- **No face shape keys.**

## Animations

Standing, singing (head raised, one hand moving), turning to leave.

## Budget

About 6k triangles; a 512 texture set.

If a later chapter brings him close, he is rebuilt at full detail then.
