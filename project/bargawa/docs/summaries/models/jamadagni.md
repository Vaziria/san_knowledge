## Build

- **MPFB body**: male, about 70, 1.66 m, thin, low muscle, upright. Asian mix.
- **Pieces**:
  - long white hair in a gelung (hair cards)
  - a long white beard (hair cards)
  - a plain white or ochre robe over one shoulder, whose hanging part gets one chain of four bones
  - barefoot
- **The japamala** is a prop attached to his left hand ([props.md](props.md)).

## Face shape keys

Blink, speech, calm with eyes closed (semedi), anger (S3), pleased (S4).

## Animations

- **Sitting**: semedi with slow breathing; sitting on the pendopo's raised place.
- **Gestures**: speaking (one hand); the order (pointing at a son); the curse (a raised palm); the boons (a hand held out).
- **Moving**: stand up, walk.
- **Dead**: lying on the steps, a held pose.

## Budget

About 12k triangles; a 1K texture set.
