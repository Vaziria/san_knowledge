# C05 Dewi Renuka

Part of the [model design](index.md). Look and costume: [models.md, C05](../../../../../docs/stories/ramabargawa/models.md).

## In Chapter 1

| Scene | What she does |
|---|---|
| 1 | at the pawon, feeding the wood into the luweng, jasmine in her hair |
| 2 | still on the bathing stone with the full kendi, looking across the lake |
| 3 | kneels on the ground below the pendopo's steps, head bowed, the kendi beside her |
| 4 | brought back to life, she does not remember; she offers Bargawa a bowl of food |

Her face is seen close at the lake (S2) and when she offers the bowl (S4).

## Build

- **MPFB body**: female, late 30s, 1.55 m, average build. Asian mix.
- **Pieces**:
  - hair in a sanggul, with a small cluster of jasmine that can be switched off
  - a kemben
  - a long jarik to the ankles: its hem gets a short chain of bones front and back, so she can walk in short steps
  - a selendang over one shoulder (a chain of five bones)
  - modest gold jewellery: earrings, a bracelet, a necklace
- **The kendi** is a prop ([props.md](props.md)).

## States

## Face shape keys

Blink, speech, a smile (S1), a distant longing (S2), fear (S3), gentleness (S4).

## Animations

- **Moving**: idle, a walk in short steps (the jarik), climbing the steps.
- **At the hearth**: crouch, feed the luweng.
- **Holding**: stand holding the kendi, startle.
- **Javanese**: kneel timpuh with the head bowed (S3), sit timpuh.
- **Giving**: offer the bowl (S4).

## Budget

About 16k triangles; a 2K set for the body and face, a 1K set for the costume.
