The game

A son who obeys his father at any cost loses everything he obeyed for, and his grief turns into the vow that drives the rest of his life.

Colour script

One place in seven light states. Each warm light belongs to someone and goes out with their loss. The red at the end is not warmth coming back: it is rage. From environment.md.

The seven scenes

Every Javanese line is Claude's draft, for you to check. The full lines are in docs/summaries/story.md.

1 · Esuk

S1 · beat 1 · 8 min
Morning. The whole hermitage. The family is whole.
  • Split wood at the stump: the same lift as the kapak later.
  • Bring the wood to Renuka's hearth; light Jamadagni's incense and the damar's four flames.
  • Sit on his mat: the five mats are full.
Renuka
Le, wis tangi? Gawakna kayu menyang pawon, ya.
Nak, sudah bangun? Bawakan kayu ke dapur, ya.

2 · Kendi

S2 · beat 1 · 5 min
Golden afternoon. The lake steps and the bathing stone.
  • Fetch his mother from the lake: a man is singing across the water.
  • Renuka still on the stone; on the far bank, Citrarata against the low sun.
  • He calls her, she starts, the kendi spills. He carries it up for her.
Renuka
Ayo mulih, Le.
Ayo pulang, Nak.

3 · Dhawuh

S3 · beat 2 · 5 min
Overcast. The pendopo, the five mats.
  • Four brothers refuse in turn and are cursed, still as stone.
  • The order comes to him. The wind stops; only the kapak answers.
  • The long walk to his mother. Black. The broken kendi, its water soaking in.
Bargawa
Sendika dhawuh, Rama.
Saya laksanakan, Ayah.

4 · Anugrah

S4 · beat 2 · 4 min
Moonlit night. One flame of the damar.
  • He asks for his mother and brothers back; the boons are granted.
  • They do not remember. Renuka offers him food; he can only put it down.
  • His mat stands apart from the others.
Bargawa
Rama, kula nyuwun Ibu gesang malih…
Ayah, saya mohon Ibu hidup kembali…

5 · Awu

S5 · beat 3 · 5 min
Ash. The gate, the burned pendopo, the shrine.
  • Home from the forest: smoke. He drops the firewood; a broken tombak lies in the gate.
  • The pendopo burned, the lamp knocked over, the pen empty. No wind, no birds.
  • Scattered beads lead to his father on the shrine steps.
Bargawa
Rama…
Ayah…

6 · Udan

S6 · beat 3 · 6 min
Night rain. The lakeside pyre, the banyan.
  • Sets the kapak on the stump; carries four logs to the shore.
  • Carries the last flame from the luweng through the rain: walk too fast and it gutters.
  • Kneels among the banyan's roots. Only the rain is left.
Narration
Udan tumurun, kaya langit melu nangis.
Hujan turun, seakan langit ikut menangis.

7 · Sumpah

S7 · beat 3 · 2 min
Red dawn. The stump, the lake's edge, the gate.
  • Takes up the kapak where the ordinary axe stood in S1.
  • The vow, tiny at the edge of the dark lake.
  • Walks out through the gate, towards beat 4.
Bargawa
Kabeh satria ing jagad iki bakal tak tumpes.
Semua satria di dunia ini akan kutumpas.

Where the scenes happen

Pertapan Jatisrana from above, drawn from the coordinates in scripts/common/jatisrana.py. North is up; one grid square is 10 m.

  1. 1Esuk: wakes in the sons' hut; the stump, the pawon, the sanggar, the pendopo.
  2. 2Kendi: down the steps past the banyan to the bathing stone; Citrarata on the far bank.
  3. 3Dhawuh: the five mats in the pendopo; Renuka below its steps.
  4. 4Anugrah: the pendopo at night, one flame.
  5. 5Awu: in through the gate; the beads lead to the sanggar.
  6. 6Udan: the woodpile, the pawon's embers, the pyre on the shore, the banyan.
  7. 7Sumpah: the stump, the lake's edge, then out through the gate.

The player walks the clearing, the paths, the steps and the shore. The forest edge and the water stop him, and the gate opens only in scene 7.

The Mobile renderer runs on both targets. It has no volumetric fog, so mist is height fog plus soft cards. Indirect light is baked once per state; the sun or moon stays live for shadows. Cloth swings on short bone chains (SpringBoneSimulator3D), not real simulation.

Open questions

The design runs on the proposal in brackets until you say otherwise.

  1. The curse: what happens to the brothers in the Javanese telling you know? [they go still as stone until scene 4]
  2. Renuka brought back: does she remember? [no; only Bargawa remembers]
  3. Beat 3: where are Renuka and the brothers when Jamadagni is killed? [away; Bargawa comes home to an empty hermitage]
  4. Why Jamadagni is killed: unexplained, or a line about the king's men and the livestock? [only the broken kandhang shows it]
  5. The Javanese lines: please check every one in docs/summaries/story.md.
  6. The title: "Ramabargawa. Bab 1: Jatisrana", or another name?
  7. Animation: Mixamo for the common moves plus Blender for the Javanese gestures, or everything keyed in Blender?
  8. Ramabargawa's skin: natural brown or ash-grey?

After acceptance

The game implementation, in this order. Each step ends with something you can see.

1 · First walk ✓
Godot 4.7.2 in tools/, the game/ project, run.ps1 game jatisrana. A stand-in figure walks the hermitage in S1 on this PC: 108–128 fps on average, 1% low 90–108, about 440k triangles and 340 draw calls in view.
2 · First character
MPFB tested on Blender 5.2; Ramabargawa's body, costume and rig in Godot, walking. The face test: expressions as shape keys.
3 · Scenes 1–2
Touch and keyboard controls, tap and hold, dialogue with subtitles from one table; the other people as grey stand-ins.
4 · Light states
S1–S7 in Godot, and the story variants built in Blender: burned pendopo, scorched banyan, pyre.
5 · The whole chapter
All seven scenes, the other characters, fire, rain, ash, and sound.
6 · Preview build
A Windows build for you to play, then your critique or acceptance, then the Android build.
