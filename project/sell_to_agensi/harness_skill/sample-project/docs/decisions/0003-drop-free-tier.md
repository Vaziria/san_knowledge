# 0003 Drop the free tier

Date: 2026-09-14. Status: accepted.

## Context

4,100 free sign-ups since June and 38 paid upgrades (0.9%). Free accounts opened 61% of support tickets.

## Decision

Replace the free tier with a 14-day trial of Pro. Existing free accounts keep 10 invoices a month until 2027.

## Consequences

The pricing page and the landing copy change: see [pricing](../product/pricing.md#why-there-is-no-free-tier) and [landing copy](../marketing/landing-copy.md#pricing-section).
