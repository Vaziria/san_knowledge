# Pricing

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Solo | $9 a month | 30 invoices a month |
| Pro | $19 a month | unlimited invoices, reminders, custom branding |
| Team | $49 a month | Pro for 5 seats |

## Annual discount

Annual plans get two months free (17% off). Monthly plans get no discount.

## Why there is no free tier

The free tier was dropped in September: 4,100 sign-ups, 38 upgrades. The reasoning is in [decision 0003](../decisions/0003-drop-free-tier.md). A 14-day trial of Pro replaced it.
