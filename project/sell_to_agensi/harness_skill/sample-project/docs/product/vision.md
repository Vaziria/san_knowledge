# Product vision

Freelancers lose hours every month writing invoices and chasing clients. Paperboat makes both take minutes.

## Who it is for

Solo designers, developers and writers who send 5 to 40 invoices a month. Agencies are out of scope until 2027 (see the [roadmap](roadmap.md#later)).

## What we promise

- An invoice in under a minute.
- Paid faster: card and bank transfer on every invoice.
- No awkward follow-ups: [payment reminders](../specs/payment-reminders.md) go out for you.
