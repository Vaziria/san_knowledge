# User interviews, August 2026

## Who we talked to

14 freelancers: 6 designers, 5 developers and 3 writers, each sending 5 to 40 invoices a month.

## What hurts

Chasing late payments came up in 12 of 14 interviews. "I hate writing the third email."

## What they would pay for

Reminders and faster payouts, not more invoice templates. This shaped the [payment reminders](../specs/payment-reminders.md) spec.
