## What works

**Every animal is the user's own low poly model** ([rules.md, Animals](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#animals)): the three.js page under "# Shape Reference" in its spec, its numbers kept as the user wrote them in `<animal>Model.ts`, one `unit` making it its real size, every face flat and painted as the page paints it, in the page's colours mapped onto the felt theme ([rules.md, Animals](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#animals) rule 5). Standing still, each is its model to 0.0000 mm, but where noted below. The user's choices when asked: the penguin into the animals, the bear on all fours, the lion at the lake meeting (and the fish in three kinds: [objects.md](objects.md)).

**What every animal shares** ([Animal.ts](../../src/figures/animals/Animal.ts)):
- The ten animals (penguin, cat, wolf, deer, bird, bear, fox, snake, frog and lion) do what their specs name: `Jump()`, `Hold(figure)`, `Walk()`, `Run()` and `Speech(text)`, and the penguin `Flap()`. They also have `Stop()`, which only the frog's spec lists.
- `Walk()` and `Run()` move the animal forward along its +z at `speed`; turning it steers ([rules.md, Moving parts](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#moving-parts) rule 7). A jump stays inside the rig and never moves the animal.
- `Hold()` puts the figure in the mouth, turned across it and scaled down to the animal's `hold` size; the penguin holds it in front of its belly, its flippers round it.
- `Speech()` shows the shared bubble ([SpeechBubble.ts](../../src/speech/SpeechBubble.ts)): at most nine words a bubble, longer text in bubbles one after another, drawn on the overlay so the Kuwahara filter never smears it. It scales with the animal's height, 0.5 to 4 times the penguin's, unless the animal sets its own size. Beyond the specs, `Speech(text, speaker)` heads each bubble with the speaker's name, and `speechBubble` hands the bubble to a camera; the lake meeting uses both ([lake_meeting.md](lake_meeting.md)).
- Each preview (`animal()` in [previews.ts](../../src/previews.ts), the penguin's its own) greets, jumps, carries the fish (a salmon), walks half a lap and runs one round a circle sized to the animal, then comes back the next time round. The Behaviours tab has a button per behaviour; Hold offers the fish, the fishing rod and the MIDI keyboard. Every animal's parts can be previewed one at a time (`?part=`; [control_panel.md](control_panel.md)), and the Figures tree lists them all under Animals, the penguin first.

**The four-legged ones** share [Quadruped.ts](../../src/figures/animals/Quadruped.ts), each built from its model (a `ModelBuild`): a lofted body and neck, a head of flat faces with what its page adds to it, a lofted tail and four legs that bend at two of their rings ([Leg.ts](../../src/figures/animals/Leg.ts)), in a four-beat walk and a gallop worked out from the legs' length. The body sinks as far as the legs need to reach their feet, so planted feet land and lift on time, and a swinging foot rises no higher than lets its leg fold without folding shut ([rules.md, Animals](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#animals) rules 3 and 4).

## Known issues and left for the user

- **Feet slide while stopping from a gallop**, on every four-legged animal: the stride shrinks while feet are down, so a planted foot moves up to 5 mm a frame on the cat, 9 on the wolf, 12.5 on the fox, 21 on the bear, 24 on the deer and 43 on the lion (at 120 fps), until it stops. The shared rig's, found on 2026-09-26; not fixed.
- **Hind legs bend a little from their models at rest:** the pages stand their front paws 1–2.5 mm higher than their hind ones, and the rig plants all four, so the hind legs are up to 2.5 mm from the model (the bear's 6 mm, the lion's 0.2).
- **The cat's hind hock brushes 1.1 mm under the ground** for a moment mid-swing at a gallop: its model's leg is long above the hock, and its hip is only 127 mm up there.
- **Kept as the models have them:** the frog's feet stand 1.5 mm off the ground and its hands and belly 6.7 mm, and its front legs slant in though its page's comment says "splay outward"; the bird's back toe sits 8 mm up its shank and its tail tips down though its comment says up; the snake's eyes stand off its head; the deer's and others' faces as the pages have them.
- **Changed from a model:** the penguin's left eye is its right one mirrored: its page turns both eyes the same way, which sinks the left 52° into the head. A line to undo if the user wants the page exactly.
- **Muted colours:** felt has no vivid blue, green, pink or red, so the bird is a muted blue-grey, the cat's eyes olive and its nose a dusty pink, and the snake's tongue orange-red. A new theme role would bring any of them nearer the page.
- **Whiskers are lines,** as the pages draw them: a pixel wide at any distance, and unlit, so they don't dim at night.
- **The penguin** crept 4.9 mm a loop round its demo circle (as the old one did), and its tail's root opens up to 7 mm as it wags (the model's tail isn't joined to its body).
- **`animal()`'s demos reach past the sun's shadow**, which the stage fits to the figure when it is shown (the snake's needs 1.2 m, has 1.13; the wolf 2.17 against 1.7): a `bounds` covering the circle would fix it for all. Not changed.
- **Unused code:** no animal uses the rig's smooth `Build` any more (with the bear's `upright` standing and `faceted` look), nor `Leg`'s rounded legs and bird's claws; they are still in the code.
- **`Stop()` is not in the specs** of the penguin and the animals the user specified, though the code and the previews have it. Neither is the speaker's name on `Speech()`.
- **Not committed:** all of it is only in the working tree; commit 278ad67 still has the smooth animals and the penguin in `src/figures/Penguin/`.
