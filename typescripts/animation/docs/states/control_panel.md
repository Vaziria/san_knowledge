## What works

- **Settings flow one way.** [settings.ts](../../src/settings.ts) holds every setting in one zustand store. The tabs in [Panel.tsx](../../src/panel/Panel.tsx) read it with `useSettings` and change it only through its setters, and `apply()` in [main.tsx](../../src/main.tsx) hands every change to [Stage.ts](../../src/Stage.ts). Two ways back: a drag away from the picked direction sets the camera to free, and a story claiming the camera sets it to the figure's own. See [rules.md, Control panel](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#control-panel) rules 1–5.
- **The URL.** Each setting has its own parameter: `?story=`, `?terrain=`, `?figure=`, `?environment=`, `?kuwahara=` (0 = off), `?camera=`, `?walkSpeed=`, `?eyeHeight=` and `?time=`, and `?part=` for one part of the figure. An unknown `?part=` shows the whole figure and is taken out of the URL. An old `?theme=` is ignored and left in it. Only settings that change are written, so an unpicked figure follows `defaultFigure` (the penguin) and an unpicked environment is the figure's own. Unknown values fall back to the defaults, and `free` is never written.
- **Figures tab:** a searchable tree ([SearchTree.tsx](../../src/panel/SearchTree.tsx), [figureTree.ts](../../src/panel/figureTree.ts)) of the `previews` table in [previews.ts](../../src/previews.ts), each figure in the folder of its spec; a figure named otherwise than its spec names it in `SPECS` (the fish's kinds share `Fish/Fish.md`, and show in a Fish folder). The arrow keys move and open folders, Enter picks, typing goes on in the search box, and Enter there picks the first match. The tree rests while a story plays.
- **Parts** ([parts.ts](../../src/parts.ts)): a figure with parts opens in the Figures tree to list them, as its fields name them (`head`, `left-front-leg`); 34 figures have 126 parts between them. Picking one, or `?part=`, shows only that part, in its place on the figure, in the pose of the figure's first frame, without the parts inside it, standing still, framed from the figure's view direction, with the shadow fitted to it. The search finds "bear head" and "head", and "bear" still finds only the bear. The Behaviours tab says a part's behaviours are the whole figure's; the camera directions go round a part as round a figure (checked with top). The part table is type-checked against each figure's fields. [rules.md, Project](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#project) rule 6, Control panel rule 8.
- **Terrains tab:** a searchable tree like the Figures tab's ([terrainTree.ts](../../src/panel/terrainTree.ts), built by [specTree.ts](../../src/panel/specTree.ts)) of the `terrains` table in [terrains.ts](../../src/terrains.ts): grass, mud, water, sand and swamp. Picking one, or `?terrain=`, shows it on its own on bare land in place of the figure; picking a figure shows the figure again and drops `?terrain=`. While a terrain shows, the Environments list rests with a note, the Behaviours tab says it has none, and the Figures tree picks nothing and says why. A story plays over it, and the tree rests. The terrains themselves: [environments.md](environments.md). [rules.md, Terrains](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#terrains) rule 9.
- **Behaviours tab:** one button per behaviour the shown figure's spec names, with a menu or a text field for each argument, from `Preview.behaviours` through [behaviours.ts](../../src/behaviours.ts). The first one run stops the demo. Restart demo builds the preview again and keeps the view. A value the figure can't read shows its error under the row. A figure without behaviours gets a note instead. Rule 7.
- **Story tab:** none, `piano_play` or `lake_meeting` ([stories.ts](../../src/stories.ts)). A story brings its own figures and environment, so the Figures and Environments tabs rest while it plays. The lake meeting's chat under it: see [lake_meeting.md](lake_meeting.md) and [streaming.md](streaming.md).
- **Environments tab:** floor, lake or grass, with the figure's own marked. A pick stays when the figure changes. Under it, Time of day (cycle, day, night), which only the lake follows.
- **Camera tab** ([CameraTab.tsx](../../src/panel/CameraTab.tsx), [camera.ts](../../src/camera.ts)): the View menu has the figure's own view, eight sides, top, walk, and `free (dragged)` after a drag. A direction keeps the preview's target and distance, and the camera glides there in about half a second. A drag of more than half a degree makes the view free, so picking the same direction again moves back. Rule 9.
- **Walking** ([walk.ts](../../src/walk.ts)): `walk`, or Walk from here, walks the camera in the first person at eye height over the ground or the lake's still water. It stops a meter short of the land's end and has no collision. It has a pad of eight buttons and keys (W S or ↑ ↓, A D, Q E or ← →, R F, Shift runs 2.5 times as fast) that work while no field, menu, slider, tab or tree has the focus. Speed (0.5–10 m/s) and Eye height (0.2–10 m) are sliders. A drag turns the view and the wheel steps half a meter. Rule 11.
- **Settings tab:** the Kuwahara switch and Radius slider (1–12 px, greyed out while off, on at 5 px by default). There is no Theme menu: `felt` is the only theme in [theme.ts](../../src/theme.ts), and `main.tsx` shows everything in it (`defaultTheme`; see [rules.md, Colour and theme](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#colour-and-theme)).
- **Audio and Stream tabs,** and the stream's hidden renderer page: see [streaming.md](streaming.md).
- **The panel itself:** shadcn/ui on Base UI with Tailwind (`src/panel/ui/`, and the blocks in [controls.tsx](../../src/panel/controls.tsx)). It is fixed at 24 rem, opaque, top right, with vertical tabs on its right edge. It folds to its rail when the open tab is clicked again, or with Collapse. The open tab and the folding are kept in `localStorage`, not the URL. Tabs stay mounted, so typed values and a search survive a switch. Menus drop below their trigger. Rule 8.
- **The stage** ([Stage.ts](../../src/Stage.ts)):
  - `show()`, `setCameraDirection()`, `setTimeOfDay()` and `setDrawing()` do nothing when the value is unchanged.
  - A new figure or environment rebuilds the figure and its environment, and frees the old geometries and materials, props included. Only a new figure moves the camera, and not while it walks.
  - It fits the sun's shadow to the figure, or moves it with the view for `followShadow`. It brings the near plane in to 2 mm for tiny figures.
  - While the view is the figure's own, it follows a story's shots. When the story claims the camera back it takes it, cutting from more than 6 m away.
  - A preview with `press()` can be played by pointer: the piano keys in [piano_play.md](piano_play.md).
  - A negative first delta is clamped to 0. See [rules.md, Checking the result](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#checking-the-result) rules 4–5.
- **Effects** ([kuwahara.ts](../../src/effects/kuwahara.ts), [overlay.ts](../../src/overlay.ts)): the Kuwahara filter runs in an `EffectComposer` on a 4-times multisampled target. Its radius is a uniform, the CSS pixels times the pixel ratio and at most 24 device pixels, so the slider changes it live. `keepSharp()` lets thin details through. Speech bubbles are on overlay layer 1, drawn after the filter. There is no tone mapping (`applyRendererTheme`). See [rules.md, Effects](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#effects).

## Known issues and left for the user

- rules.md's Control panel rule 3 list of URL parameters leaves out `?time=`, `?walkSpeed=` and `?eyeHeight=`, which are in rules 11 and Environments 22.
- `figureTree.ts` repeats the folder logic (`className`, `foldersOf`) of [specTree.ts](../../src/panel/specTree.ts), which the Terrains tree uses. One could build on the other once the terrain work settles (task 16 is done).
- `setKuwahara()`, `setWalk()` and `setMoves()` in Stage.ts have no "unchanged" check (rule 2). They only store numbers and set a uniform, so calling them on every change does no harm.
- The first time a figure brings a new light count (the firefly's point light), the whole scene recompiles: 0.37 s on the floor, 1.8 s on the lake. Left for the user in [task 01](../../../../tasks/done/01-firefly-figure.md): keep a dark point light in the scene, or compile the next scene first with `renderer.compileAsync`.
