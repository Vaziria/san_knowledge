## What works

- **Three environments,** `floor`, `lake` and `grass`, in the `environments` table in [previews.ts](../../src/previews.ts). The Environments tab or `?environment=` picks one. Until then a figure shows in its own (`figureEnvironments`: the boat, the dock and the fish's three kinds on the lake; the trees, the cliff, the cloud and the fog on the grass; the rest on the floor). The stage moves the scenery so the figure's spot is at the origin, puts the environment's fog in the scene, calls its `update` first each frame and frees it with the figure ([Stage.ts](../../src/Stage.ts)). Each environment gives the walking camera its ground's height and reach (`Environment.ground`). Detail: [rules.md, Environments](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#environments) rules 1–10.
- **Floor** ([Floor.md](../../src/environtments/Floor/Floor.md), [Floor.ts](../../src/environtments/Floor/Floor.ts)): one 20 m plane in `scene.floor`. No water, so the boat rests on it. No sky, border, weather or night.
- **Grass** ([Grass.md](../../src/environtments/Grass/Grass.md), [Grass.ts](../../src/environtments/Grass/Grass.ts)): 30,000 instanced blades 2–4 cm tall, thick in the middle and thinning out over 4 m, on ground a shade darker that runs 40 m out and fades into the sky there. It has the border and the sky (below) but no wind or weather: its clouds drift toward +x, its sky never greys, and its blades and border grass stand still. Rule 12.
- **The lake's land and water** ([Lake.md](../../src/environtments/Lake/Lake.md), [Lake.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Lake.ts), [Ground.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Ground.ts), [Water.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Water.ts)):
  - A lake about 18 m across and 1.4 m deep with a 0.3 m bank. Beyond it, uneven land out to 45 m (humps, then low hills from 9 m out), a mottled lawn with the soil texture over it, never lower than 8 cm above the water, except where the swamps are dug into it (below).
  - Figures stand at `Lake.LANDING` on the +z shore, kept level for `clear` m round it: 2.2 m, or 5.6 m for the lake meeting ([lake_meeting.md](lake_meeting.md)).
  - The water is a see-through sheet moving in three crossing waves 1.2–3 cm high. `heightAt` is exactly what is drawn, so the boat rides it; `keepDry` ([water.ts](../../src/environtments/water.ts)) keeps it out of the hull; [Splash.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Splash.ts) throws up drops and rings where the fish breaks it. It leaves out its cells over land the waves never reach (7.8 cm at most), half of its triangles, so no lake water shows in the swamps dug below its level.
  - Rules 3–7, 9, 14, 15 and 20.
- **What stands and grows on the lake's land,** all placed by seeded generators, so the lake is the same on every load, and built in an order that keeps earlier things where they were:
  - 217 stones in 6 instanced meshes ([Stones.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Stones.ts)) and four boulders placed by hand ([Boulders.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Boulders.ts)).
  - Nine trees of the tree figures' kinds and two autumn ones, a maple and an oak, none near the landing or on its +z side ([Trees.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Trees.ts); the trees themselves in [trees.md](trees.md)).
  - Five mud pits on nearly flat land ([MudPits.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/MudPits.ts); the pit itself in [objects.md](objects.md)).
  - A sward of short tufts, mown on the landing ([Sward.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Sward.ts)), and 82 patches of the grass figures ([Meadow.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Meadow.ts), [grasses.md](grasses.md)). The grass keeps off the boulders, pits and stones.
  - `Lake.obstacles()` gives anything walking the trunks, boulders and big stones to go round (the lake meeting's animals). The swamps' pools need nothing there: they lie under 12 cm, which the meeting's land takes for water.
  - **A dock, on a lake built with `LakeOptions.dock`** (only the lake meeting's; task 09). [DockSite.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/DockSite.ts) searches the shore from the angle asked for until it finds a site:
    - its deck is off the boulders, mud pits and trees, and off whatever the caller keeps it from (the fish);
    - the way onto it is 2.5 m of dry land;
    - its deck stands over the land under its level part.

    Its landward end is 1.2 m past the top of the bank. The dock may turn up to 20° from running straight out.
    - **Its site is cleared:** the stones in its way or on the way onto it go (`Stones.takeAway`), and so do the sward's tufts and the meadow's plants under it. Everything else keeps its place.
    - **Its deck is ground to walk on:** `Lake.deckAt()` is in the environment's `ground.heightAt`, for the walking camera and the lake meeting. It eases from the land's height over the first 0.3 m, so there is no step.
    - `Lake.siteDock()` does the same for a lake built without one: the dock's own preview ([objects.md](objects.md)).
  - **Logs floating in the water, on a lake built with `LakeOptions.logs`** (only the lake meeting's; task 11). [Logs.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Logs.ts) builds four round logs ([objects.md](objects.md)) of seeded sizes, after the dock.
    - **Each finds a home** where all of it, however it drifts and turns, stays over water deep enough to keep 25 cm off the bed, 40 cm off the boulders and the dock (`nearDeck()` in [DockSite.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/DockSite.ts)), and 30 cm off what the caller keeps it from (the fish).
    - **It floats** 45% of its trunk's thickness down, easing its height, pitch and roll toward the water toward either end and at its sides, updated in `Lake.update` after the waves. It rocks more in wind.
    - **It drifts:** up to 0.5 m downwind in a lasting wind and back when it drops, a slow eddy, and a little swing, always within 0.71 m of its home.
    - `Logs.contains()` says whether a point is inside one, where it has drifted to (the meeting's cameras keep out of them).
  - **Swamps at the lakeside** ([Swamps.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Swamps.ts)): the swamp terrain, dug into the land past the top of the bank, sited after everything else, which keeps its place.
    - **Three on the default lake,** 5–6.6 m along the shore and 5–5.5 m across. One is on the far shore to the southwest, against the boulder there, where the preview cameras look; the others are either side of the landing, 9 and 13 m from it. The lake meeting's wider clearing leaves room for two: the far shore's and the one right of the landing.
    - **Their water** stands 4–10 cm over the lake's, in hollows dug 20–25 cm into the land. `Lake.groundAt()` is the land as dug. The pools are 16–22 cm deep at most and cover a quarter to a third of each swamp.
    - **Their sites are cleared** of stones (none lay inside one) and of grass. A tree or a boulder may stand at a swamp's undug rim; its plants keep off them.
    - **The lake's ground is left out under them** wherever a swamp covers half the ground or more, and its water wherever the waves never reach.
    - Their edges fade into the lawn, their water dulls at night and in rain, and their plants and waves move in the lake's wind.
  - Rules 11, 13, 16, 18, 24 and 26.
- **Border and sky, on the lake and the grass** ([Border.ts](../../src/environtments/Border.ts), [haze.ts](../../src/environtments/haze.ts), [Sky.ts](../../src/environtments/Sky.ts), [sun.ts](../../src/sun.ts)): cliffs 9–24 m high with open stretches between, 560 mist puffs drifting round the edge, 4,500 tufts of tall grass, and the land fading into the sky's colour over its last 14 m. Above, a dome deepening from `scene.background` at the horizon to `zenith`, the sun drawn where the key light comes from (`SUN`), and 60 clouds drifting downwind. The camera sees 3 km. Rules 19 and 21; the cliff, cloud and fog objects are in [objects.md](objects.md).
- **Weather at the lake** ([Weather.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Weather.ts), [wind.ts](../../src/environtments/wind.ts), [Rain.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Rain.ts)): fog (first at 25 s), windy spells (first at 8 s) and showers (first at 40 s, 40–90 s long, 2–4 min apart), each on its own seeded timer. The wind sways the sward, meadow, tree crowns and border grass on the graphics card and roughens the water. A shower is 3,000 streaks in a box round the camera and 280 rings on the water, both kept sharp ([rules.md, Effects](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#effects) rule 8); it dims the light a step, greys the sky and dulls the glint. There is no setting for any of it. Rules 17 and 25.
- **Day and night, at the lake only** ([DayNight.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/DayNight.ts)): 6 min of day, 1 of dusk, 4 of night and 1 of dawn, the first dusk a minute in. The Time of day setting (`?time=cycle|day|night`, on the Environments tab; see [control_panel.md](control_panel.md)) holds it at day or night. The stage mixes its background and lights by `dayNight.dim` every frame (`applyDaylight()`) from the theme's `scene.night` (`sceneAt()` in [theme.ts](../../src/theme.ts); [rules.md, Colour and theme](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#colour-and-theme) rule 3). The sun sets, and the fog, the land's fade, the border and the water follow. Twenty fireflies ([Fireflies.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Fireflies.ts)) come out at dusk round the landing, are hidden by day, and put their lanterns out in rain; the firefly itself is in [objects.md](objects.md). Rules 22 and 23.
- **Terrains,** the kinds of ground an environment's land can be put together from ([rules.md, Terrains](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#terrains)), in `src/environtments/terrains/` with shared [parts.ts](../../src/environtments/terrains/parts.ts). Only the swamp is used by an environment: the lake's swamps (above).
  - [GrassTerrain](../../src/environtments/terrains/GrassTerrain.ts) ([spec](../../src/environtments/terrains/GrassTerrain.md)): a mottled lawn under about 3,700 tufts 7–26 cm tall that bend in a wind.
  - [MudTerrain](../../src/environtments/terrains/MudTerrain.ts) ([spec](../../src/environtments/terrains/MudTerrain.md)): wet mud with a sheen, drier patches and edge, and 4–5 puddles.
  - [WaterTerrain](../../src/environtments/terrains/WaterTerrain.ts) ([spec](../../src/environtments/terrains/WaterTerrain.md)): the lake's waves on a sheet that is clearer where shallow, over a bed that deepens toward the water's colour, with a wet band on the bank.
  - [SandTerrain](../../src/environtments/terrains/SandTerrain.ts) ([spec](../../src/environtments/terrains/SandTerrain.md)): 3 cm of sand in soft drifts, covered in wind ripples from the sand texture ([sand.ts](../../src/textures/sand.ts)), in the theme's `sand` role ([theme.ts](../../src/theme.ts)).
  - [SwampTerrain](../../src/environtments/terrains/SwampTerrain.ts) ([spec](../../src/environtments/terrains/SwampTerrain.md)): still, murky water in the land's low places, with duckweed on 30% of it, hummocks of peat with a sedge tussock on each, 60–90 clumps of cattails in the shallows, and wet mud, then mossy peat with short grass, round the water. It is dark all over: the moss is the grass colour × 0.38, the mud the floor colour turned halfway to the wood's brown (× 0.11 wet, × 0.2 in the bare patches), and the water the water colour turned 70% toward the grass, × 0.17. Like the water, it needs a hollow (on flat land it is only its ground and plants). Its waves die away in the shallows, so its waterline stays crisp. It lies 3 cm deep, not 1.2 cm, so the bare land's mesh stays under it. An environment can give the colour its edge fades to (`rim`) and dull its water at night and in rain (`setNight`). [rules.md, Terrains](../../../../docs/3d_modelling/rules.md#terrains) rule 8.
  - Each lies over the land an environment gives it (`heightAt`), where its `mask` says (an uneven oval filling its footprint by default), 1.2 cm above the land (the sand and the swamp 3 cm) and tucked 1.5 cm under it at the edge; water fills the land's hollows. Each is sized and seeded, and answers `heightAt`, `covers` and `update`.
  - Each is shown on its own on bare land ([Land.ts](../../src/environtments/terrains/Land.ts)) under the sky, in a light breeze, from the panel's Terrains tab or `?terrain=` ([terrains.ts](../../src/terrains.ts); the tab in [control_panel.md](control_panel.md)).

## Known issues and left for the user

- **The swamps at the lakeside are hard to see from the preview cameras.** The two near ones stand 9 and 13 m to the sides of the landing, outside a preview's frame, and the far one is 25 m off, behind the boulder and trees. Turn or walk the camera to see them (`?camera=walk`).
- **At the swamps:** no rain rings fall on their pools, and the fireflies fly over the land as it was before it was dug. The lake's ground mesh crosses a swamp's surface in its lawn-coloured outer edge (up to 2.1 cm where the swamp covers 30–50% of the ground), where the two may shimmer.
- The terrains other than the swamp are built but no environment uses them: the lake keeps its own ground, water, sward and mud pits. Moving it onto them would change its look where they differ: the grass terrain's lawn is darker than the lake's (0.6 against 0.75 of the grass colour) and its tufts lighter, and the water terrain is clearer in the shallows. The water terrain has none of the lake water's splashes, night or rain.
- `GrassTerrain` borrows the tuft shape from the lake (`tuftGeometry` in [Sward.ts](../../src/environtments/Lake/Sward.ts)); it belongs with the terrains, which would mean changing the lake's files. Tufts may lean up to 6 cm past a grass terrain's footprint.
- No mud pit shows from the preview cameras. The two placed in their frames are 20 m off, hidden by the grass and the land's rise; walk the camera (`?camera=walk`) to see one. Task 04 asks which rule to relax: a pit on the bank beside the clearing, one just behind the figure, or a taller or darker rim.
- The pits' puddles don't dull at night as the lake's water does; they only darken with the moonlight (task 04). Still so: `Lake.update` hands the pits nothing.
- No moon and no stars (task 02). No rain sound, and the ground doesn't get wet (task 06).
- The stream at night: task 05-stream saw the night setting reach the stream's hidden page, but nobody has looked at the streamed picture at night (task 02).
- The weather has no setting. To watch a shower, wait 40 s after the lake loads or set `scenery.weather` from a test page; fog and wind can't be caught in a screenshot without shortening the times in `Weather.ts` for it (rules 17 and 25).
- Seen in the code: the fireflies keep 1.8 m from `Lake.LANDING` whatever the clearing, so at the lake meeting (5.6 m) they fly inside the clearing, among the animals.
- The terrains' bare land (`Land.ts`) has rings 25 cm apart: land with creases or hollows tighter than about 2.6 m pokes up through a terrain lying 1.2 cm on it. The tab's lands are smooth enough; an environment built from terrains will need the same care, or finer land.
