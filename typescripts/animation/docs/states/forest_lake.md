## What works

- **The forest_lake environment** ([ForestLake.md](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md), [ForestLake.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.ts)): a valley round a lake, laid out as the reference's overview, picked as `forest_lake` in the Environments tab or with `?environment=forest_lake`, in **four seasons**: the tab's Season setting under it (spring, summer, autumn, winter; `?season=`, spring by default) picks which. A new season rebuilds the forest lake in about 1.6 s and keeps the view; its own assets shown in it follow the season.
  - **Its own look**, not the theme's. Its colours are a palette picked from the references (`PALETTE` in [parts.ts](../../src/figures/ForestLake/parts.ts)), which each season changes (`palette(season)`: the grass, needles, leaves, blossom, moss, flowers, lily pads, the tent's canvas, winter's paths). Each season brings its own sky colour and lights (`ForestLake.light(season)`: a warm sun and a sky light pale blue from above and softened ground colour from below), through `Environment.light` in [previews.ts](../../src/previews.ts), which [Stage.ts](../../src/Stage.ts) uses in place of the theme's. Every lit material of the forest lake keeps colours vivid where the light is dim (`VIVID` in parts.ts), as the reference paints its shade.
  - **One sun leads** (since [tasks/lighting.md](../../../../tasks/done/lighting.md): "ambient light is too strong", "shadows are too weak", "ground is very uniformly bright", "no strong light hierarchy"): the sky light is 0.95 in spring, summer and autumn and 1.1 in winter (1.6 and 1.75 before), the sun 2.95, 3.05, 2.9 and 2.5 (2.65, 2.75, 2.6 and 2.2), about three times the sky light. The ground's baked light keeps 48% of the light in a tree's shadow (60% before), and right at the foot of a trunk, a rock or a bush 52% (72%), fading out by its foot's reach as (1 − d/foot)^1.6 (squared before): `SHADED` and `CONTACT` in [Terrain.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Terrain.ts). Rocks from 0.3 m across cast it (over 0.7 m before). The grass is patched lighter and darker in two sizes (about 20 m and 6 m), its darks deeper. The scatter's copies take the ground's baked light, so grass, flowers and tufts darken with it.
  - **A thin haze** (`FOG` in [ForestLake.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.ts)): linear from 10 m to 360 m toward the horizon's colour (from 70 m before, so nothing in the valley was hazed): the cliffs 40–50 m off about a tenth of the way, the far forest a fifth or more. The backdrop's hills and mountains have their haze painted in, as before.
  - **The seasons**, as the reference's four draw the one valley:
    - spring: fresh green, cherry blossom, pink water lilies, flowers of every colour, cherry petals falling round the trees by the landing (on the island the crystal tree's sparkles drift instead, in every season);
    - summer: deeper green, the cherries in green leaf, white lilies, white and yellow flowers;
    - autumn: the broadleaf trees orange, red and gold, the cherries red, some pines golden larches, gold grass with fallen leaves drawn thick over it (the ground's shader) and lying in drifts and on the lake, leaves falling round the trees by the landing, the tent orange, more mushrooms, a warmer haze;
    - winter: snow on the ground and on every asset's upturned faces (`snowOn()` in parts.ts), icicles on the cliffs' top, the tower's eaves and over the cave, bare trees among the pines, berries in the snow, the lake ice (plates of pale ice with white cracks, frosted toward the shore) but for a hole of open water round where a floating figure goes, the rivers ice, the falls nearly still, snow falling, a paler sky.

    - [layout.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/layout.ts): the map, with the lake's and the island's outlines, the rim of the heights with their level along it, the rivers, the stream and its cascade, the paths (the way in from the south through the forest entrance's gate, new), the landing, and where each landmark stands; how much bigger the cave and the ruins stand than their assets (`CAVE_SCALE` 1.3, `RUINS_SCALE` 1.25). Since [task 21](../../../../tasks/done/21-forest-lake-wider-lake-crystal-island.md) the lake is about 52 m across east to west (x −26.3 to 25.5) and 43 m north to south: everything west of the landing moved up to 6 m west (all of it from x −10, fading out north of z −17, so the small waterfall and the northern cliffs stayed) and everything east of the main waterfall up to 6 m east (all of it from x 13), each landmark with its shore; the island is 16.3 × 16.8 m, grown 1.7 times north and east from its south-west.
    - [Terrain.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Terrain.ts): the land's height from the map, sampled 0.6 m apart round the lake and wider apart out to 340 m, coloured by the season (`GROUND`), with the sun's shadows and the hollows' darkness baked in and a grain drawn per pixel. The island is a plinth of rock straight up out of deep water; the lake's banks are steeper. A trench is cut under the cave. Its fine sampling reaches x ±68 (±62 before task 21), and the hills rise from further out east and west. `colorAt()` is the ground's colour as meshed.

## Known issues and left for the user

- **It is heavy:** 1.9 to 2.3 M triangles and 1.2 to 1.5 s to build, each time it is shown (a new figure or season rebuilds it). Smooth on the RTX 3050; weaker graphics cards were not tried. Built a piece at a time (`ForestLake.build`, the forest lake meeting's next season), Landmarks and the scatter's placement still take 0.15–0.3 s each in one go: their constructors would have to stop between their parts.
- **What swims is faint:** the water hides up to 72% of what is under it, as the lake's does.
- **Contact shadows are the ground's only** ([tasks/lighting.md](../../../../tasks/done/lighting.md)): baked into the ground mesh round trunks, rocks and bushes. The water under the bridges and piers gets none (their undersides are darker now, lit by less sky light), and a figure's or an animal's shadow is the sun's, which the stage casts only round what the camera looks at.
- **The cave's inside is lit by its torch alone,** baked in.
- **No wind, day and night or weather** (winter's snowfall aside).
- **Paths:** the references' wooden stairs and cliff path are not built (stone stairs and log steps are, where a path climbs steeply); the heights are reached only by the path round the east, and the heights trail runs on from it to the footbridge. The heights path's last 2 m run down the cliff over the cave: nothing is laid there, and its dirt colour still runs down the cliff.
- **The stone paths** are a draft from the sheet ([StonePath.md](../../src/figures/ForestLake/StonePath/StonePath.md)). Which kinds each path is made of (`PATH_KINDS`) and where the trails run are Claude's, from [task 20](../../../../tasks/done/20-forest-lake-varied-paths.md)'s defaults.
- **The trails:** a pine standing more than 1.5 m off the heights trail still reaches over it at eye height here and there. The forest lake meeting's land ends 39 m from its middle, so its animals walk only the first 7 m of the wreck trail and none of the heights trail.
- **One puddle:** only one dip along the paths' middles is deep enough (1.5 cm under the ground 1.5 m either way) to hold one.
- **The crystal tree's trunk is hollow** to the forest lake meeting's sight (only its surface is solid), so a camera could stand inside it; none goes near it, since the island is out of the animals' reach. Its sparkles barely show by day.
- **The wider lake, the island's size and the ages' shares** are this task's defaults (12 m wider, 1.7 times across, about one in six young and one in ten old).
- **Previews:** the assets' previews on the floor are lit by the theme's light, not the forest lake's.
- **The winter hole:** the open water a floating figure needs is a round hole in the ice west of the island, which the reference's winter doesn't have.
- **The wreck:**
  - Its levelled site cuts about 1.3 m into the rising land behind its tail, where its furrow starts climbing the hill to the west.
  - Not built from its sheets: the tail sheet's two thruster pods and side fins (the whole wreck's sheets show only the fin there), separate "overgrown" variants and the small-parts library as pieces of their own, and the sheets' line-drawing (vector and edge) views, which draw the same model rather than a look of its own.
  - Its preview on the floor frames the whole debris field, so the wreck is small in it; the parts' previews are closer.
  - The hold's and the cockpit's light is baked: they look lamp-lit even where the sun would reach in.
