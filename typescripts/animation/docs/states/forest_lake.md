## What works

- **The forest_lake environment** ([ForestLake.md](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md), [ForestLake.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.ts)): a valley round a lake, laid out as the reference's overview, picked as `forest_lake` in the Environments tab or with `?environment=forest_lake`, in **four seasons**: the tab's Season setting under it (spring, summer, autumn, winter; `?season=`, spring by default) picks which. A new season rebuilds the forest lake in about 1.6 s and keeps the view; its own assets shown in it follow the season.
  - **Its own look**, not the theme's. Its colours are a palette picked from the references (`PALETTE` in [parts.ts](../../src/figures/ForestLake/parts.ts)), which each season changes (`palette(season)`: the grass, needles, leaves, blossom, moss, flowers, lily pads, the tent's canvas, winter's paths). Each season brings its own sky colour and lights (`ForestLake.light(season)`: a warm sun and a sky light pale blue from above and softened ground colour from below), through `Environment.light` in [previews.ts](../../src/previews.ts), which [Stage.ts](../../src/Stage.ts) uses in place of the theme's. Every lit material of the forest lake keeps colours vivid where the light is dim (`VIVID` in parts.ts), as the reference paints its shade.
  - **The seasons**, as the reference's four draw the one valley:
    - spring: fresh green, cherry blossom, pink water lilies, flowers of every colour, cherry petals falling round the trees by the landing and on the island;
    - summer: deeper green, the cherries in green leaf, white lilies, white and yellow flowers;
    - autumn: the broadleaf trees orange, red and gold, the cherries red, some pines golden larches, gold grass with fallen leaves drawn thick over it (the ground's shader) and lying in drifts and on the lake, leaves falling round the trees by the landing, the tent orange, more mushrooms, a warmer haze;
    - winter: snow on the ground and on every asset's upturned faces (`snowOn()` in parts.ts), icicles on the cliffs' top, the tower's eaves and over the cave, bare trees among the pines, berries in the snow, the lake ice (plates of pale ice with white cracks, frosted toward the shore) but for a hole of open water round where a floating figure goes, the rivers ice, the falls nearly still, snow falling, a paler sky.
  - **Built in modules** in `src/environtments/ForestLake/`:
    - [layout.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/layout.ts): the map, with the lake's and the island's outlines, the rim of the heights with their level along it, the rivers, the stream and its cascade, the paths (the way in from the south through the forest entrance's gate, new), the landing, and where each landmark stands; how much bigger the cave and the ruins stand than their assets (`CAVE_SCALE` 1.3, `RUINS_SCALE` 1.25).
    - [Terrain.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Terrain.ts): the land's height from the map, sampled 0.6 m apart round the lake and wider apart out to 340 m, coloured by the season (`GROUND`), with the sun's shadows and the hollows' darkness baked in and a grain drawn per pixel. The island is a plinth of rock straight up out of deep water; the lake's banks are steeper. A trench is cut under the cave.
    - [Water.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Water.ts): its own shader for the lake, the rivers and the stream, coloured by the season, with ice in winter (`iceAt()`: waves stop under it, `surfaceAt()` is still there), two waterfall sheets (slowed and paler when frozen, no mist) with foam, and splashes.
    - [Cliffs.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Cliffs.ts): columns of darker grey rock stacked up the cliffs all along the rim, mossy on top, with boulders at their feet and in the falls' pools; their ledges and top edge (`ledges`) are where bushes and grass grow; snow and icicles in winter.
    - [Backdrop.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Backdrop.ts): the sky dome, 26 drifting clouds, rings of hills and snowy mountains, and land out to the horizon, all unlit and hazed, coloured by the season (`SKY`: autumn's hills orange-brown, winter's white with the mountains snowy to their feet).
    - [Scatter.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Scatter.ts): what grows and lies about, instanced, 9,000 to 12,000 copies by season: a mixed forest of pines (three tones), round broadleaf trees and cherries, thick down to the banks (the far ones and those over 30 m from the landing simpler), bare trees in winter; bushes, also on the cliffs' ledges; tall grass, flower clusters, low tufts and single flowers; a ring of boulders along the lake's shore and round the island, rocks along the stream and in the forest, pebbles and flat stones in the paths; reeds in the shallows; lily pads (not in winter); logs, stumps and mushrooms; autumn's fallen leaves. Each copy is tinted by the light on its ground and a little in brightness and hue.
    - [Landmarks.ts](../../src/environtments/ForestLake/Landmarks.ts): the piers, the boat, the bridges, the tower, the cave, the ruins, the camp (now with a pot over the fire), the forest entrance's gate with fences off its posts, the lamp posts, sign posts (two with lanterns) and fences, and the island's things (its cherry tree, a pine, a bush, flowers, a fence round two sides, two double lamps, a sign and stone steps up from its jetty, which the walking camera climbs), each in the season. What never moves is merged; flames, lights, glows, the flag and the boat stay live.
  - **Figures stand at its landing**, a level clearing on the south shore, looking north across the lake; a figure in the water (the boat, the rowboat, the fish) goes to open water west of the island (in winter the hole in the ice) and rides its waves. The walking camera walks the land, the water and ice, the decks, the island's steps and the cave's floor, within 75 m.
  - **What moves**: the water, the waterfalls, the stream, the flames, sparks and lanterns, the flag, the clouds, the season's falling petals, leaves or snow, and the moored boat (frozen still in winter). There is no day and night, weather or wind.
  - **For the forest lake meeting** (since 2026-09-28; [forest_lake_meeting.md](forest_lake_meeting.md)), nothing drawn changed:
    - `ForestLake.landAt()`: the ground to stand on without the water (the land, the decks, the cave's floor); `groundAt()` is it and the water.
    - `Landmarks.piers`: each pier's landward end, heading, size and deck height.
    - `Landmarks.solid`: the merged scenery and the boat.
    - `Scatter.solid`: the trees', bushes', rocks', logs' and stumps' instanced meshes. `Scatter.low` is the bushes, logs and stumps of them, which `Scatter.obstacles` (trunks and rocks) leaves out.
- **Its assets, 29 figures** in `src/figures/ForestLake/` (the Figures tab's ForestLake folder), each with its spec and its parts to preview one by one. The fence came first, approved by the user.

## Known issues and left for the user

- **It is heavy:** 1.9 to 2.3 M triangles and 1.2 to 1.5 s to build, each time it is shown (a new figure or season rebuilds it). Smooth on the RTX 3050; weaker graphics cards were not tried.
- **What swims is faint:** the water hides up to 72% of what is under it, as the lake's does.
- **The cave's inside is lit by its torch alone,** baked in.
- **No wind, day and night or weather** (winter's snowfall aside).
- **Paths:** the references' stone steps, wooden stairs and cliff path are not built; the heights are reached only by the path round the east.
- **Previews:** the assets' previews on the floor are lit by the theme's light, not the forest lake's.
- **The winter hole:** the open water a floating figure needs is a round hole in the ice west of the island, which the reference's winter doesn't have.
