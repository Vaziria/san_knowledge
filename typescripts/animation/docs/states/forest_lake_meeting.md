## What works

The viewers of the YouTube live stream meet at the forest lake as its own animals, as they meet at the lake in the [lake meeting](lake_meeting.md). Spec: [forest_lake_meeting.md](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/forest_lake_meeting.md), a draft. Code: [forest_lake_meeting.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/forest_lake_meeting.ts) and [ForestMeeting.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/ForestMeeting.ts), listed in [stories.ts](../../src/stories.ts) after `lake_meeting`; `?story=forest_lake_meeting`.

- **It plays as the lake meeting plays, on its modules** in `src/story/lake_meeting/`: the roaming ([Roam.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/Roam.ts), [Land.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/Land.ts), [Member.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/Member.ts)), the host's view ([HostView.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/HostView.ts)), the quiet camera's follow ([Follow.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/Follow.ts)) and the fish's own leaps ([FishLeaps.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/FishLeaps.ts)). ForestMeeting.ts is Meeting.ts's turns and close-ups, copied, with the forest lake's cast, land, sight and fish: a fix to one of the two may be wanted in the other.
- **The dev server keeps it from the same chat as the lake meeting** ([meeting.ts](../../meeting.ts) with the forest's rules, [events.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/events.ts); [chat-bridge.ts](../../chat-bridge.ts), [streaming.md](streaming.md)). Every chat message goes to both meetings; each has its own seven bots and its own viewers' animals. Its events are the Vite event `forest-meeting:event`, and a page asks `GET /__chat/forest-meeting` first ([chat.ts](../../src/chat.ts)). A built page, with no dev server, makes 7 bots of its own.
  - **Its kinds:** the forest lake's wolf, deer, fox, boar, rabbit, squirrel, duck, otter and frog ([forest_lake_animals.md](forest_lake_animals.md)); `!wolf` and the rest switch. A lake kind (`!cat`) is only a comment there.
  - **Its commands:** `!jump`, `!walk`, `!run` and `!stop`, as at the lake, and each kind's tricks, from its sheet's poses (`TRICKS`): `!howl` (a wolf), `!flap` (a duck), `!sit` and `!look` (a fox), `!look` (a deer), `!eat` (a boar or a rabbit), `!stand` (an otter), `!wave` (the host). The dev server sends a trick only for a kind that has it.
  - **The host is the explorer**, the channel owner's. The owner can't switch him.
  - **The dragon is the first viewer's to comment** while it is no one's (`MeetingRules.special`, `DRAGON` in events.ts): their first message brings them the dragon in place of an animal, whatever kind they asked for (`!wolf hi` says "hi"). It takes no bot's place, isn't one of the 12 viewers' animals (so the FIFO never takes it), and can't be switched; it takes no commands. They keep it until they leave, quiet for 10 minutes or removed by a moderator; then it is no one's until the next new viewer's first message (a viewer already there with an animal doesn't get it). A dev server restart keeps whose it is. The lake meeting has no special kind.
- **The season changes every minute** (since [task 18](../../../../tasks/done/18-forest-meeting-season-change.md); [season_change.md](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/scenarios/season_change.md), the spec's item 2). It starts in the Environments tab's Season (`?season=`, spring by default; `Story.environmentIn`, used by [main.tsx](../../src/main.tsx)). After 60 s of the meeting's own time (`SEASON_TIME`, counted in its `update`, so a page left in the background doesn't catch up) the next comes: spring, summer, autumn, winter, then spring again. The forest lake itself is in [forest_lake.md](forest_lake.md); shown with a figure it keeps the season picked.
  - **Built ahead, swapped in.** The story gives the stage `Preview.changes` ([forest_lake_meeting.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/forest_lake_meeting.ts), [previews.ts](../../src/previews.ts), [Stage.ts](../../src/Stage.ts); [control_panel.md](control_panel.md)). The next season's forest lake is built a piece at a time between frames (`ForestLake.build`) and the meeting measures its land and sight from it (`prepare`: `forestGroundBuild`, `Land.build`, `ForestSight.build`). Its shaders are compiled, and at the minute it swaps in. It waits while the fish is in the air, since in winter the only open water is the ice hole: 0.6–0.7 s once or twice in ten minutes.
  - **The meeting carries on** (`take()`): every animal stays where it is, facing the same way, doing what it did. The members, the bubbles, the turns waiting, the camera and its shot, the bots and the fish go on, and the host isn't sent back to the landing. The new land, sight (asked through `seeing`, so `Follow` and `HostView` carry on) and the fish's water and splash come in.
  - **Stepping out** (`Roam.reland`, [Roam.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/Roam.ts)): one the new season puts inside something or over water walks to the nearest spot where it fits (as much room as a roaming spot keeps), without stopping for others, and is held once out if its turn to talk or `!stop` came meanwhile. One on its way whose way is no longer clear finds a new one. 0–5 a change, nearly all into and out of winter, when the bare trees come and go.
  - **Coats** ([guests.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/guests.ts), `coatOf`): a wolf or a boar whose id picks the coat wears snow in winter, a deer its autumn or winter coat, painted where it stands (`SetColor`); the others keep their colour, and their figure.
  - **A season picked while it plays** comes the same way, at once (2.7 s to build and measure it, pressed), and the next minute counts from there; picking the one shown only starts its minute again (`pick`). The stream's renderer page follows a pick, and keeps its own season through the panel's other settings.
  - **The change is a cut:** the scenery, the sky, the lights and the fog in one frame.
- **The cast** ([guests.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/guests.ts)): the forest lake's animals and the explorer as their sheets made them, each in a small subclass that gives it what the meeting asks of a figure (`Figure` in [kinds.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/kinds.ts)); nothing in their own files changed.
  - A speech bubble just over its head, headed by the viewer's name, sized for its height as the lake's animals' are.
  - `Walk()` and `Run()` start it going only when it isn't already (the meeting asks every frame, and the frog's hop would start over each time); a jump or a flap under way ends first.
  - `Jump()` is a hop where it stands, so it never lands anywhere the meeting didn't put it: the four-legged ones spring straight up (their rig's leap, 18–40 cm), the frog leaps as its sheet has it, the duck flaps its wings, the explorer jumps.
  - Its colour is one of its sheet's variations, picked from the viewer's id, so the same on every page and in every season; its season's coat instead half the time where its sheet has one (snow, winter, autumn: `coatOf`). Before task 18, an odd pick ran over a list with the season's coat in it, so it could land on it, or on another plain colour than in the other seasons.
  - **The frog hops level** (since [task 18](../../../../tasks/done/18-forest-meeting-season-change.md)): its sheet sets its feet down on level ground, and leaned with the slope while it hopped, it stood mid-hop for good on a slope of 0.1 across its way. It is leaned after.
  - Standing at a spot, it now and then does one of its tricks rather than hop (`Pastime`, through `Roam`'s new pastime hook): the wolf howls, the fox sits or looks up, the boar and the rabbit eat, and so on. The squirrel and the frog only hop.
- **The land** ([ground.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/ground.ts)): the land round the lake out to 45 m from its middle (39 m before [task 21](../../../../tasks/done/21-forest-lake-wider-lake-crystal-island.md), which widened the lake 12 m; the camp, the ruins, the gate and the cave within it), the piers' and bridges' decks and the cave's floor its ground (`ForestLake.landAt`), a shore pier's first 0.6 m eased up from the bank.
  - **Water:** the lake, the rivers and the stream, and their banks up to 12 cm over them (`Land`'s new `wet` option).
  - **Not the island:** only land the smallest animal can walk to from the landing counts (`Land`'s new `home`).
  - **What stands on it:** the trees' trunks and the rocks as circles (`Scatter.obstacles`), and the footprints of the rest found from their meshes: whatever of the landmarks, the cliffs, the bushes, the logs and the stumps is 6 cm to 1.3 m over the ground under it, each 25 cm square of it a circle. That takes in the tent, the fences, the lamps, the piers' posts, the bridges' rails, the ruins and the cave's rock.
  - **The paths' steps** (the stone stairs since [task 19](../../../../tasks/done/19-forest-lake-stone-path.md), the log steps since [task 20](../../../../tasks/done/20-forest-lake-varied-paths.md)): the land is measured on the ground under them (`landAt(x, z, false)`), since a tread's 15 cm edge on its half-meter cells counted as too steep, and its animals stand on them up a ramp through the middles of the risers (`ForestLake.rampAt`), within half a riser of each tread. The stone paths' flagstones, at most 5 cm high, and the stepping stones, gravel and roots, at most 4 cm, are walked over. So the land is the same cell for cell as before them.
  - **The trails** (since [task 20](../../../../tasks/done/20-forest-lake-varied-paths.md)): what stood on the forest lake's two new trails is gone, so nothing the animals go round is left on either trail's walked strip. The land ends 45 m from its middle, and the wreck trail moved west with the camp, so it still takes in the wreck trail's first 7 m and none of the heights trail; in winter 73 cells by the wreck trail's start have more room.
  - **The two shore piers** are decks, as the lake meeting's dock is: one walk in ten may go out on one, for the animals that fit.
- **The cameras' sight** ([ForestSight.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/ForestSight.ts), on the new `Seeing` interface in [Sight.ts](../../src/story/lake_meeting/Sight.ts)):
  - **The floor:** the ground mesh sampled every 25 cm, or the water now: the lake's waves (its ice in winter), the rivers and the stream, 8 cm more over water.
  - **What is solid:** cubes of 25 cm, kept as bits, from 1 m under the ground to 28 m over it, over a square 64 m each way round the lake's middle (17 m and 58 m before [task 21](../../../../tasks/done/21-forest-lake-wider-lake-crystal-island.md): the island's crystal tree is 22 m tall, and its crown reaches out over the lake's bed). A cube is marked where the landmarks, the trees, bushes, rocks, logs and stumps, or the cliffs pass through it. A path's pebbles, under 30 cm across, are left out.
  - The next season's is measured a piece at a time (`ForestSight.build`, every 4000 triangles or 16 rows), as is its land.
- **The forest spirit dragon** ([Dragon.ts](../../src/story/forest_lake_meeting/Dragon.ts); the figure in [forest_lake_animals.md](forest_lake_animals.md)), the spec's item 14, a draft. It is the first viewer's to comment (above), and says what they write; it takes no commands.
  - **Talking:** a speech bubble headed by the viewer's name, the meeting's as the animals' are (the figure and its spec unchanged), 1.7 m over its head's pivot wherever its neck puts it, straight up however it banks, and 11 times the lake penguin's, about 4 m wide, so it reads from the camera on it. Their comments take turns with everyone's. As the turn begins it is held (`hold`): round the tree it keeps going round, neither roaring (the meeting starts the turn before it looks for a roar) nor going down to the meadow; on the ground it stands still; landing or taking off, it carries on. The camera cuts to it wherever it is and follows it exactly (`talkShot`), from 1.5 m under its bubble's tip so that, standing, all of it is in the picture under its words. It films from the first of six places that stays out of everything solid, with a meter round it, over the ground or water, and sees it for the next 2 s: ahead of it looking back at its face, to one side or the other (round the tree, the side away from it first), then from behind, about 10 m off (at most 11.1 m from its bubble's middle, which reads from 11.5 m). Every 0.3 s it checks that place again and picks again if it is no longer clear. Its turn claims the camera back as a comment does. A viewer who leaves takes their turns and its bubble with them.
  - **Round the crystal tree:** 16 m out from its trunk (±0.6 m), going round from the east by the south: its crown reaches 10.75 m out at its widest (18–20 m up), so with the wings' 3.6 m there is a meter to spare at any height, and the shores are clear above about 8 m. It flies 10 m over the water to the west and 20 m to the east, over the east shore's trees and cliffs (up to 16 m), rising and falling 1.5 m besides, banking into the turn. It starts there, flying (`startFlying()`).
  - **Roaring,** every 40–80 s of its time round the tree, only over the open water to the west (within 1 rad of due west) and on its circle (within 2 m). When nothing is shown and nothing waits, its roar is a turn of its own: the camera cuts to it and follows it exactly for the roar (3.6 s), from the first of four places round it (ahead of it looking back, from inside or outside the circle, behind) that stays out of everything solid, with a meter round it, and sees it, all through the roar, where it will be: foreseen by steering it as it steers, 0.1 s at a time (foreseen along its circle at constant radius, the camera went 3–13 m off and into the north-east cliffs). That turn takes the camera from no one who has moved it, and the quiet is still counted through it, so the host's view and the follow go on as before. With the chat's turns showing or waiting, it roars unseen; with no camera having room for 30 s, too.
  - **The meadow:** after 2–4 minutes round the tree, on the south side, it flies to a gate over the landing's south (9 m from the landing), lines up and comes straight on south, 9 m over the ground, and lands once it is as far from its touchdown as landing takes (`landingDistance`): 33 m south of the landing, in the open ground kept for the landing's view south, beyond where the animals roam (the land's 45 m from the lake's middle reaches 24.6 m south of the landing there). It walks about there 35–60 s, to spots at least 4 m apart, its middle kept 31.5–38 m south and within 6 m either side, turning at most 0.3 rad a second, standing still 4–8 s at six spots in ten; then it turns on round to face the lake, takes off over the landing and flies back round the tree. On the ground it stands on the meeting's ground (each foot on it, `ground`), leaning with its slope over 3 m either way.
  - **The cameras** see it as one of the animals (its `member`, 4.8 m round, on its way whenever it moves): the follow may follow it (flying, from behind as well), the host's view and the turns' close-ups keep out of it and don't look through it. The sun's shadow is 6 m tall (`bounds`), so all of it casts on the meadow.
  - **The cameras' sight** counts what is outside its square as solid now: filming the dragon at the meadow's south end, the follow stood in a trunk past it.
- **The fish,** a trout, swims round 1.4 m from the open water's middle north of the landing (in winter the hole in the ice). For a supporter's leap it first turns to face its circle's middle, then leaps across the open water. The lake meeting's heights, 0.3–1.5 m by the Super Chat's tier, become 0.3–1 m, the most the forest fish leaps. Its own leaps (0.15–0.28 m, every 20–60 s) go the way it swims. The camera films its leap from the shore's side, nearer than the lake's, the trout being 40 cm long.
- **Turns** as at the lake. A trick's animal stops as its turn begins, so the camera finds it facing the way it chose to film it from, and its turn lasts the trick (2.6–6 s).
- **The Story tab** lists it (hinted "starts in the Season picked in Environments; a new season every minute") and shows the chat controls under it, with its host and commands, and that the first viewer to comment while the dragon is no one's gets it ([ChatControls.tsx](../../src/panel/ChatControls.tsx), [control_panel.md](control_panel.md)).

## Known issues and left for the user

- **The big animals can't cross the outlet's bridge or walk the piers.** The wolf, the deer and the boar take up half their length round them, more than a 1.7 m bridge or a 1.9 m pier leaves room for. The fox, otter, rabbit, squirrel, duck, frog and the explorer fit. A big one that lands past the bridge (on the east shore, by the cave) stays on that side.
- **Each page roams on its own**, as at the lake: only who is there and what they say is shared. The dragon too: each page has its own, going round and down to the meadow at its own times, so its viewer's words may be filmed over the lake on one page and in the meadow on another.
- **The dragon is seen mostly when the camera goes to it:** for its viewer's comments, for its roars (when the chat is quiet) and when the quiet camera happens to follow it, one animal among the eight or so.
- **A long comment holds the dragon long:** a 300-character comment is some 25 s of bubbles, during which it neither roars nor goes down to the meadow. It flies 10–20 m up, above the opening shot's and the host's view's frames, and its meadow is behind them. A roar takes no camera when the chat is busy, and none while it is in the meadow, where it only walks.
- **The meadow is the only ground with room for it:** the land the animals roam, the island and the shores are too crowded with trees, rocks and landmarks for a dragon 7.9 m long. So it always comes down at the same spot, 33 m south of the landing.
- **Two long frames a minute:** the next season's landmarks (about 0.27 s) and the scatter's placement (about 0.16 s) are built in one piece each, about 3 and 4 s after a change. Everything else is built in slices under 25 ms. Making those two stop between their parts means reworking their constructors (Landmarks.ts, Scatter.ts), which task 18 said not to do.
