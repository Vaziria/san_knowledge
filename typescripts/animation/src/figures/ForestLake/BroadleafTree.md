# Broadleaf Tree Figure.

Draft written from the user's later reference sheets for the forest lake (the "Tree (Broadleaf)" in its four seasons, and the round green trees mixed in with the pines round the lake), for the user to correct. One of the forest lake's assets (the forest_lake environment, [ForestLake.md](../../environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md)); its colours are the forest lake's own palette ([parts.ts](parts.ts)), picked from the images, not the theme's.

A round leafy tree: a straight orange-brown trunk flaring into its roots and forking at about 40% of its height into five or six branches rising up and out into a big round crown. A clump of leaves sits at each branch's end, a big one fills the crown's heart and another tops it; each clump has smaller clumps standing out of it, so the crown is lumpy rather than smooth.

Low poly, every face flat shaded, bright and saturated, as the reference is. The leaves are light where they face the sky, a warm light here and there, and dark green in the shade underneath, each face its own shade.

Size: 6 m tall by default, its crown about 4.5 m across.

Seasons: fresh light green in spring, deep green in summer, and in autumn orange, red or yellow (`tone`), as the reference's autumn mixes them. In winter it is bare: each branch ends in a few twigs holding clumps of snow, and snow lies along the branches' tops.

Options: `height`, `season` (spring by default), `tone` (its colour in autumn: orange by default, red or yellow), `simple` (plainer clumps and fewer twigs, for trees seen from afar), `seed`.

Parts: the trunk (with its branches and roots), the crown.

No animation behaviour: it stands still. (In autumn, in the forest lake, leaves fall from the ones near the landing; the leaves are the environment's.)
