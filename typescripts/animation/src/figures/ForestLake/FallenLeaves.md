# Fallen Leaves Figure.

Draft written from the user's later reference sheets for the forest lake (autumn's "Fallen Leaves (Ground)", and the leaves lying on the ground and the water in the reference's autumn), for the user to correct. One of the forest lake's assets (the forest_lake environment, [ForestLake.md](../../environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md)); its colours are the forest lake's own palette ([parts.ts](parts.ts)), picked from the images, not the theme's.

A patch of autumn leaves lying on the ground, thickest in its middle and thinning out: each a small flat leaf with five pointed lobes round a darker middle, orange, red, gold or rust, turned every way, some overlapping.

Low poly, every face flat shaded, bright and saturated, as the reference is.

Size: 1.6 m across by default; each leaf 10 to 16 cm.

Seasons: autumn's only. The forest lake scatters them in autumn, and floats some on its lake by the shore; the ground there has more drawn over it, and leaves fall from the trees.

Options: `radius` (0.8 m by default), `count` (26 by default), `seed`.

One part, the leaves.

No animation behaviour: they lie still. They lie at y 0.
