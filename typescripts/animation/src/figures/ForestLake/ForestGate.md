# Forest Gate Figure.

Draft written from the user's later reference sheets for the forest lake (the "Forest Entrance", a wooden gate over the path into the valley), for the user to correct. One of the forest lake's assets (the forest_lake environment, [ForestLake.md](../../environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md)); its colours are the forest lake's own palette ([parts.ts](parts.ts)), picked from the images, not the theme's.

A wooden gate over a path: two tall square posts either side of it carry a heavy crossbeam, its ends running out past them and cut aslant, the top running further; a tie beam spans the posts under it, and a brace stands in each top corner. A lantern hangs from the crossbeam just inside each post, over the path, a soft glow round it, and grass grows round the posts' feet. The posts stand along x, the path running through along z.

Low poly, every face flat shaded, bright and saturated, as the reference is. The wood is the fence's, with the grain split along it; the lanterns are the lamp post's.

Size: the posts 3.1 m over the ground and 0.24 m square, 3 m apart by default; the crossbeam 4 m long.

Seasons: in winter snow lies along its beams and on the lanterns' roofs.

Options: `width` (between the posts' middles; 3 m by default), `season` (spring by default), `seed`.

Parts: the frame (the posts, beams and braces), the lanterns (their iron), the glass, the tufts.

The lanterns flicker a little on their own. No behaviours.
