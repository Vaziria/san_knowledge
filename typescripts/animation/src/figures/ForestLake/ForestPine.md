A pine: tiers of lime-green needles stacked into a cone, four by default, the lowest widest and the top one a narrow spire. Each tier bulges a little at a shoulder on its way up and hangs in a skirt of long drooping points with shorter ones between. Under them, a short orange-brown trunk flares into its roots.

Low poly, every face flat shaded, bright and saturated, as the reference is. Each tier is lightest at its tip, darker down to its skirt and darkest underneath; the bark is in two shades, stripe by stripe.

Its tiers are big and overlapping, their points drooping, as the later sheets draw them.

Size: 7 m tall by default, about 4.6 m across its lowest tier.

Seasons: It keeps its needles all year, a deeper green in summer; in winter snow lies on its tiers, white on top and blue-white on their slopes, the green showing under them.

Options: `height`; `tiers`; `simple`, six points to a tier and no shoulders or roots, for pines seen only from afar (the forest lake's far hills); `season` (spring by default); `tone`, green by default, or dark, light, or gold (a larch in autumn, as the reference's autumn forest has among its pines); `seed`.

Parts: the trunk (with its roots), the crown.

No animation behaviour: it stands still.
