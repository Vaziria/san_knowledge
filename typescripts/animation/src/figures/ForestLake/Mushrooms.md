# Mushrooms Figure.

Draft written from the user's later reference sheets for the forest lake (the "Mushroom Cluster", and the mushrooms on the log and at the stump's foot), for the user to correct. One of the forest lake's assets (the forest_lake environment, [ForestLake.md](../../environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md)); its colours are the forest lake's own palette ([parts.ts](parts.ts)), picked from the images, not the theme's.

A few mushrooms growing together on a patch of moss, with a tuft of grass beside them: the biggest upright in the middle, the others round it, leaning out a little. Each has a pale stem, a little wider at its foot, and a domed cap, pale gills showing under its rim; most caps are red spotted with white, some plain tan.

Low poly, every face flat shaded, bright and saturated, as the reference is.

Size: the biggest 20 cm tall with a cap 20 cm across; the group about 60 cm across.

Seasons: in autumn leaves lie round them; in winter snow sits on their caps and the moss is snow. (The forest lake has none in winter.)

Options: `count` (5 by default), `season` (spring by default), `seed`.

Parts: the mushrooms, the dressing (the moss and grass, and the leaves in autumn).

No animation behaviour: they stand still. The same mushrooms grow on the log and at the stump's foot (addMushroom in [parts.ts](parts.ts)).
