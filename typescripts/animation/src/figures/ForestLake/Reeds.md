# Reeds Figure.

Draft written from the user's later reference sheets for the forest lake (the "Reed / Grass" and the cattails in "Grass & Flower", and the reeds along the lake's shore), for the user to correct. One of the forest lake's assets (the forest_lake environment, [ForestLake.md](../../environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md)); its colours are the forest lake's own palette ([parts.ts](parts.ts)), picked from the images, not the theme's.

A clump of reeds standing up out of the shallows: sixteen long, narrow blades, each folded along its middle, rising and arching over at their tips, and among them a few taller straight stems, each topped with a cattail's velvety brown head, rounded at both ends, and a thin spike above it.

Low poly, every face flat shaded, bright and saturated, as the reference is. The blades are darker at their feet and lighter toward their tips; the heads are two browns, darker at their ends.

Size: 1.15 m tall by default; the heads 19 cm long.

Seasons: deeper green in summer, golden brown in autumn, and dry straw in winter, with snow on their heads.

Options: `height`, `cattails` (how many stems carry a head; 4 by default), `season` (spring by default), `seed`.

Parts: the blades (with the stems), the heads.

No animation behaviour: they stand still. They stand at y 0, the water's surface or the ground; their feet go 15 cm under it.
