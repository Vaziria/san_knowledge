# BermudaGrass Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

Bermuda grass (Cynodon dactylon, rumput grinting): a low mat of runners creeping over the ground, with short, flat leaves and short upright shoots at their nodes, and thin flower stems ending in four or five finger-like spikes.

Size: about 0.2 m tall and 0.9 m across by default. Every grass takes its size in meters: `new BermudaGrass({ height, width })`. With only `height`, it keeps its shape; a wider plant grows more leaves and stems, not only longer ones. `seed` grows another plant of the same kind.

No animation behaviour yet: it stands still.
