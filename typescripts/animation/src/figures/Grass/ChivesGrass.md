# ChivesGrass Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

Chives (Allium schoenoprasum): a clump of hollow, round, upright leaves tapering to a point, and stiff flower stalks topped by round pom-poms of florets in the theme's flower colour.

Size: about 0.45 m tall and 0.3 m across by default. Every grass takes its size in meters: `new ChivesGrass({ height, width })`. With only `height`, it keeps its shape; a wider plant grows more leaves and stems, not only longer ones. `seed` grows another plant of the same kind.

No animation behaviour yet: it stands still.
