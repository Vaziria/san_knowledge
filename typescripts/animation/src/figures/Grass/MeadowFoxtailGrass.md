# MeadowFoxtailGrass Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

Meadow foxtail (Alopecurus pratensis): a tuft of soft, arching leaves and slightly nodding stems, each topped by a soft, pale seed head like a fox's tail, fuller and shorter than timothy's, tapering to both ends.

Size: about 0.8 m tall and 0.5 m across by default. Every grass takes its size in meters: `new MeadowFoxtailGrass({ height, width })`. With only `height`, it keeps its shape; a wider plant grows more leaves and stems, not only longer ones. `seed` grows another plant of the same kind.

No animation behaviour yet: it stands still.
