# RedFescueGrass Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

Red fescue (Festuca rubra): a dense, fine tuft of thread-like leaves, reddish at their feet, and slender stems with loose, nodding seed heads of small reddish spikelets.

Size: about 0.55 m tall and 0.45 m across by default. Every grass takes its size in meters: `new RedFescueGrass({ height, width })`. With only `height`, it keeps its shape; a wider plant grows more leaves and stems, not only longer ones. `seed` grows another plant of the same kind.

No animation behaviour yet: it stands still.
