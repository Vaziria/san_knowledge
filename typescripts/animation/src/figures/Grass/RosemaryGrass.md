# RosemaryGrass Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

Rosemary (Salvia rosmarinus): a small bush of woody stems in bark that lean out and turn up, thick with narrow needle-like leaves in pairs, dark above and pale beneath. No flowers.

Size: about 0.8 m tall and 0.7 m across by default. Every grass takes its size in meters: `new RosemaryGrass({ height, width })`. With only `height`, it keeps its shape; a wider plant grows more leaves and stems, not only longer ones. `seed` grows another plant of the same kind.

No animation behaviour yet: it stands still.
