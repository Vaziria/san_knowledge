# TimothyGrass Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

Timothy (Phleum pratense): a tuft of flat, grey-green leaves and many stiff, upright stems, each topped by a dense, blunt, cylindrical seed head.

Size: about 0.95 m tall and 0.5 m across by default. Every grass takes its size in meters: `new TimothyGrass({ height, width })`. With only `height`, it keeps its shape; a wider plant grows more leaves and stems, not only longer ones. `seed` grows another plant of the same kind.

No animation behaviour yet: it stands still.
