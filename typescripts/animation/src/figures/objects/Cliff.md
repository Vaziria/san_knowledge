# Cliff Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

A natural rock cliff: a steep, craggy face toward the front, built of horizontal layers of rock (strata), with ledges and overhangs where one layer stands out past or back behind the next, a few vertical cracks, and a broken top edge. Behind the top the back slopes down to the ground, and at both ends the layers stop one by one, stepping down to a low shoulder. A few fallen boulders lie at its foot. The rock is the theme's stone colour, with flat faces like the lake's stones, darker in the cracks and under the overhangs.

Size: about 10 m wide, 6 m tall and 4 m deep by default. It takes its size in meters, `new Cliff({ width, height, depth })`, and is built to exactly that size. `seed` builds another cliff of the same kind.

No animation behaviour: it stands still.
