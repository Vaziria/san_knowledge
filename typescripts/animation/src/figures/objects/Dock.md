# Dock Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

A dock: a plain village jetty (*dermaga kayu*), traditional rather than modern. A walkway of wooden planks laid across three beams, with narrow gaps between the planks, runs out from its landward end over the water. The beams rest on cross-beams, each on a pair of log posts with their bark on, about every 2 m. The posts stand on whatever lies under them: the lakebed under the water, the bank near the shore. Near the shore, where the ground comes up to a cross-beam, the beam rests on the bank and has no posts. Nothing else: no railing, ladder, lamps or boats.

It is a little uneven, as a hand-built jetty is: the planks are not all as wide, sit a little askew and a few millimeters high or low, and the posts lean a little. The planks and beams are wood with its grain along their length; the posts are bark. Both are in the theme's colours.

Size: 3 m wide, 6.5 m long, and its deck 0.36 m above the still water by default. It takes its size in meters, `new Dock({ width, length, height })`, and the height of what lies under it, `bedAt`, for its posts. `seed` builds another dock of the same kind.

At the lake meeting it runs out from the far bank, across the water from the landing, and the animals walk out on it.

No animation behaviour: it stands still.
