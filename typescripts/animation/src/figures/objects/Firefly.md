A low poly firefly about 2 cm long: a black head, an orange thorax, three dark brown segments of abdomen and a glowing lantern at its tail, two pairs of see-through wings, six thin legs and two antennae. It never lands: it hovers about its own length above the ground, bobbing gently and turning a little from side to side, its wings beating, and its lantern flashes about every 2.5 s. The lake's fireflies fly about at night.

No animation behaviour: it can't talk, and it does nothing it is told, only hovers and flashes on its own.
