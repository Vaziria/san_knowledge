# Log Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

A log: a length of tree trunk, felled, cut to length and lying on its side on the ground. It is not quite straight or round: it bows a little, thins a little from one end to the other, and its girth swells and dips. It is covered in bark, and a few short stubs stick out of its upper half where branches were cut off, each from a swelling in the bark. Both ends are sawn across, a little off square, and show the end grain: growth rings round the pith, darker heartwood, a few drying cracks and a rim of bark. The stubs are sawn across too. It is bedded a little into the ground, so it lies along its whole length. A log floating in water is round underneath instead (`round`), since a flat face would show through the water: the lake meeting's logs float so. The bark is the theme's wood colour darkened into bark; the sawn ends are the plain wood colour, paler, like fresh wood.

Size: about 2.4 m long, 0.48 m tall and 0.52 m deep by default. It takes its size in meters, `new Log({ width, height, depth })`, its length being the width, and is built to exactly that size. `seed` builds another log of the same kind.

No animation behaviour: it lies still.
