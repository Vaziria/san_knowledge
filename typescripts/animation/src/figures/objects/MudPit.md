# Mud Pit Figure.

Draft written from the code, for the user to correct.

A mud pit: a shallow hollow of wet mud on the ground, its outline uneven rather than round. In the middle the mud is dark and wet, smooth, with a sheen, and one or two shallow puddles of standing water lie in it. Toward its edge the mud dries paler and rougher, its grit, small stones and cracks showing. A low, trodden rim is pushed up round it. It never goes below the ground: its mud lies at about the ground's level, and it reads as a hollow by its rim. It lies on uneven ground as the ground lies. The mud is the bare land's colour, darker where it is wet; the puddles are the water's colour, with a glint like the lake's.

Size: about 3 m across and 2.2 m deep by default, to the outer edge of its rim. It takes its size in meters, `new MudPit({ width, depth })`, and is built to exactly that size. `seed` builds another pit of the same kind. At the lake, five of them lie about the land.

No animation behaviour: it lies still.
