# Story Of Forest Lake Meeting

> Draft by Claude from your ask ("make story ForestLake Metting like lake meeting", 2026-09-28), for you to correct. Delete this line once it says what you want.

1. The viewers of our YouTube live stream meet at the forest lake, as they meet at the lake in the [lake meeting](../lake_meeting/lake_meeting.md). The chat comes from `san_youtube`, reading the live chat of the channel set in the Story tab; both meetings meet from the same chat.
2. It plays at the [forest lake](../../environtments/ForestLake/ForestLake.md), in the season the Environments tab's Season picks (spring, summer, autumn or winter), spring by default. A new season builds it again.
3. The explorer, the boy of the forest lake's animal sheets, is the host. The channel owner's messages and commands go to him. He starts on the landing and roams the land like the other animals. He is always at the meeting: he never walks off and is never destroyed.
4. A viewer's first message brings their animal: it appears with a jump at a random spot anywhere on the land. The kind is random, one of the forest lake's own animals: Wolf, Deer, Fox, Boar, Rabbit, Squirrel, Duck, Otter or Frog. `!wolf`, `!deer`, `!fox`, `!boar`, `!rabbit`, `!squirrel`, `!duck`, `!otter` and `!frog` switch it. Each wears one of its sheet's colours, the same for a viewer every time, in its season's coat where its sheet has one (a snow wolf in winter).
5. A message becomes its animal's `Speech`. Each bubble starts with the viewer's name. No name tag. An animal stops walking while its bubble shows.
6. Commands, as at the lake: `!jump`, `!walk` and `!run` (to a new spot near it, which it keeps), `!stop`. And each kind's own tricks, from its sheet's poses: `!howl` (a wolf), `!flap` (a duck), `!sit` and `!look` (a fox sits, or looks up at the sky), `!look` (a deer looks back over its shoulder), `!eat` (a boar or a rabbit), `!stand` (an otter stands up) and `!wave` (the host). An animal stops where it is for its trick. The owner's commands work on the host as everyone's do on theirs.
7. At most 12 animals, not counting the host. An animal whose viewer is quiet for 10 minutes walks away. When the limit is hit and a new one joins, the oldest (the one that joined first) is destroyed, like FIFO.
8. The Fish, a trout, swims round in the open water north of the landing (in winter the hole in the ice) and leaps out for a Super Chat, a Super Sticker or a new member. A bigger Super Chat leaps higher. It leaps across the open water, never onto the ice. Now and then, every 20–60 s, it also leaps a little of its own accord, lower than any supporter's leap and without the camera going to it.
9. A deleted message is taken out of its bubble. A viewer a moderator removes leaves at once.
10. The Story tab can send test chat messages, without YouTube.
11. The camera is the lake meeting's (its item 10): comments, commands and supporters' leaps take their turns in the order sent, the camera going to the animal (or the fish) first; it cuts when many are waiting; when the chat is quiet for a few seconds it goes back to the host, wherever he is; after 30 seconds with nothing new it follows a random animal for 30 seconds at a time, from a few angles, never through the ground, the water, the trees, rocks, bushes, fences and buildings or another animal. A comment or command takes the camera back from someone who moved it, once they let go.
12. If no one is joining, 7 bot animals of random kinds are at the meeting. Each viewer who joins takes a bot's place, and a bot comes back when a viewer leaves. Bots don't talk.
13. Every animal roams the forest lake's land, the host too: the meadows, the shores, the camp, the ruins, the paths and the forest round the lake, and now and then out on a pier. It walks to a spot, stands a while, sometimes jumps or does one of its tricks, then picks another, now and then running or going on a long walk. Each spot it reaches is its place until it walks on; it never goes back to an earlier one. It goes round the water, never into it, and round the trees, rocks, bushes, logs, fences, the tent and everything else standing on the land, and round the other animals. No animal goes to the island: there is no way there. One that leaves walks off into the forest.
