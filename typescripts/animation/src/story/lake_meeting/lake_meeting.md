# Story Of Lake Meeting

> Draft by Claude from our talk (2026-09-25), for you to correct. Delete this line once it says what you want.

1. The viewers of our YouTube live stream meet at the lake. The chat comes from `san_youtube`, reading the live chat of the channel set in the Story tab.
2. The Bear is the host. The channel owner's messages and commands go to it. It starts on the landing and roams the lake like the other animals (item 12). It is always at the meeting: it never walks off to the land's end and is never destroyed.
3. A viewer's first message brings their animal: it appears with a jump at a random spot anywhere on the lake's land. The kind is random: Cat, Wolf, Deer, Bird, Fox, Snake, Penguin or Frog. `!cat`, `!wolf`, `!deer`, `!bird`, `!fox`, `!snake`, `!penguin` and `!frog` switch it.
4. A message becomes its animal's `Speech`. Each bubble starts with the viewer's name. No name tag. An animal stops walking while its bubble shows.
5. Commands: `!jump`; `!walk` and `!run` (to a new spot near it, which it keeps); `!stop` (it stays where it stopped a while, then roams on from there); and `!flap` for a penguin. The owner's commands work on the host as everyone's do on theirs.
6. At most 12 animals, not counting the Bear. An animal whose viewer is quiet for 10 minutes walks away. When the limit is hit and a new one joins, the oldest (the one that joined first) is destroyed, like FIFO.
7. The Fish swims in the lake and jumps out (`JumpOutFromWater`) for a Super Chat, a Super Sticker or a new member. A bigger Super Chat jumps higher. Now and then, every 20–60 s, it also leaps a little of its own accord, lower than any supporter's leap and without the camera going to it.
8. A deleted message is taken out of its bubble. A viewer a moderator removes leaves at once.
9. The Story tab can send test chat messages, without YouTube.
10. When a comment pops up or a command is given, the camera follows its animal, so nothing is missed. They take turns in the order they were sent: the camera goes to the animal, then its bubble pops up or it does the command. For a Super Chat, it shows the Fish's jump first. When many are waiting, the camera cuts instead of gliding. When the chat is quiet for a few seconds, it goes back to the host, wherever it is, and follows it as it roams, from in front of it or from another side where nothing is in the way. The meeting opens on the host at the landing. After 30 seconds with nothing new (no comment, command or leap), it follows a random animal for 30 seconds, then another random one for 30 seconds, and so on, never the same one twice running, the host and the bots too. It takes each one from a few different angles (in front, beside, low, high, behind it as it walks), cutting between them, and never films through the ground, the water, the trees and rocks or another animal. The next comment or command takes the camera at once. If someone has moved the camera away (dragged it, picked a view or is walking), the next comment or command takes it back, once they let go of it.
11. If no one is joining, 7 bot animals of random kinds are at the meeting. Each viewer who joins takes a bot's place, and a bot comes back when a viewer leaves, appearing at a random spot like a viewer's animal. Bots don't talk. Bots and viewers' animals alike stay where they stop and walk on from there.
12. Every animal roams the lake's land, the host too, all round the water, out among the hills and trees: it walks to a spot, stands a while, sometimes jumps, then picks another, now and then running, and now and then going on a long walk to another part of the lake. Each spot it reaches is its place now, where it stays, facing the way it arrived, until it walks on to the next; it never goes back to an earlier place, and nothing puts it back at a fixed place at the meeting. It goes round the water, never into it, and round the trees, boulders, big stones and the other animals. One that leaves walks off to the land's end.
