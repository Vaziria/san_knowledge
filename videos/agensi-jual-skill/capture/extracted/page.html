    Give your agent new capabilities

    Your agent is capable but generic. A skill is a capability someone else already built and tested. Connect Agensi once and your agent can do new work the moment you ask for it.

    What is a skill?

    A skill is a capability your agent loads before it answers you. It hands your agent the exact steps an expert would follow, so it can do work it couldn't do out of the box.

    • →Capabilities on demand. Add something your agent was never trained on: cold emails that get replies, audits that catch the real bugs, pages that ship.
    • →No prompt engineering. The skill brings the know-how. You just say what you need in plain English.
    • →Runs inside your agent. Nothing to download or keep up to date. Connect Agensi once and your agent always runs the latest version.
    • →You approve every purchase. Your agent finds the right skill and asks before it spends a cent. Every skill is built by a vetted expert and passes an 8-point security scan.
