What the stream really shows (1280x720, three panes in one translucent terminal window over a night scene):
- Pane 1: "radio@getresolved ~" / "❯ neofetch" — a pixel-art card (sakura, red sun, snowy mountain, pagoda, torii) and an info block:
  radio@getresolved / OS Raspberry Pi OS / Host Raspberry Pi 3B+ / Shell san_radio / Tracks <count> / Now <track> / Uptime <time>
- Pane 2: "radio@getresolved ~/music" / "❯ eza --tree" — the music folder as a tree, the playing track marked with ♪ and ◀:
  .
  ├── ♪ Adventure in Forest Dungeon.wav
  ├── ♪ Broken Legendary Shrine.wav
  ├── ♪ Lake of silence and death.wav
  ├── ♪ Temple of Monk Warrior.wav
  ├── ♪ arabic.wav
  ├── ♪ auntum.wav  ◀
  ├── ♪ mistjapanese.wav
  └── ♪ ruin.wav
- Pane 3 (chat): each track is typed as "play <file>"; each live chat comment as greet @name "comment", answered "Hello, @name!":
  radio@getresolved ~
  ❯ play auntum.wav
  radio@getresolved ~
  ❯ greet @kamu "lagunya enak buat kerja malam"
  Hello, @kamu!
- Powerline prompt bars: [radio] [~] › with a block cursor.
- Clock in the tab bar.
